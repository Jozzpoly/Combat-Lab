# Combat Lab — competing whole-world discovery hypotheses (2026-10-10)

**Status:** research decision material, **NOT** an approved architecture, implementation order, PR plan, merge instruction, Owner verdict or canonical performance claim. Authored in the neutral documentation lane after the Owner requested another deep, independent preparation round. Read [current live research truth](RESEARCH_STATE.md), [Owner-first reconciliation](OWNER_TRUTH_RECONCILIATION_2026-10-09.md) and newest Owner corrections before acting. ## 0. The question to defend

> When fundamentally different physical bodies coexist with manipulable, persistent and varied matter, **do materially different actions and consequences become naturally discoverable without the author preprogramming a route, role, puzzle or victory?**

This is the Oct 7 frontier, not the narrower “how to optimize body movement,” “how to simulate clever crowd behavior,” “how to make a functioning claw,” or “how to force an LLM brain into actors.” The Owner wants to spontaneously create, observe, break and understand wildly different physical situations, not operate a curated benchmark.

**Historical human baseline:** positive narrow evidence for body-envelope/mass difference (S0/B0), collective physical pressure (R0) and persistent contact/matter (R3), alongside product-level Owner FAIL for later R0/R1/L0. The failures constrain claims even if later computer tests are green. [Failure scar](FAILURE_SCAR_2026-10-06.md), [frontier reassessment](FRONTIER_REASSESSMENT_2026-10-07.md).

**Present engineering boundary:** PR #1 and PR #2 are **isolated and unaccepted donors**. PR #1 has local tactile reaction and real matter/physics but largely primitive shared roaming and motorized forms; PR #2 offers actual independently articulated contact actions, but its other bodies are substantially passive and some beautiful A/Bs were deliberately staged. Both need real qualitative human evaluation; neither has it.

## 1. New insight from external disciplines — transfer with limits

**Affordances are relational**, not simply a catalog of features that exist in an object's data: a narrow opening, loose load or long member affords different actions depending on a body's own envelope, reach, leverage, strength, contact geometry and control. The owner-important question is therefore **body × matter × circumstance**, not “number of components.” Ecological psychology's body-scaled examples are supporting conceptual precedent, **not validation of a virtual game feel**:
- Lobo et al., *History and Philosophy of Ecological Psychology*, 2018: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6280920/
- Dotov et al., *Understanding affordances*: https://avant.edu.pl/wp-content/uploads/DDLNMW-Understanding-affordances.pdf

**Morphology can do actual control work.** Independent physical construction can change object motion before an “intelligent planner” exists, but the same motor policy may cease to work after changes in body geometry. This supports independently editable parts, real reciprocal joints, empirical co-evaluation of body and action. It does **not** entail sophisticated soft robotics on day one:
- Paul, *A basis for the analysis of morphology and control in robotics*, 2006: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0921889006000613
- Pfeifer, *Co-development of morphology and cognitive skills*, 2005: https://www.robotcub.org/misc/openday/slides/Rolf_Pfeifer_-_Co-development_of_morphology_and_cognitive_skills.pdf
- Nordmoen et al., *MAP-Elites Enables Powerful Stepping Stones and Diversity for Modular Robotics*, 2021: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8115726/

**Contact can be an action without grasping.** Nonprehensile robotics documents manipulation through friction, external environmental constraints, off-axis impact, pushing and relative motion, including bodies incapable of ordinary “hold item” actions. PR #2's clamp/null experiment is a small material instance, not a robust grasp claim:
- Stüber et al., *Let's Push Things Forward: A Survey on Robot Pushing*, 2020: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7805815/
- Lynch, *Nonprehensile Robotic Manipulation: Controllability and Planning*, 1996: https://publications.ri.cmu.edu/storage/publications/pub_files/pub1/lynch_kevin_1996_1/lynch_kevin_1996_1.pdf

**Beware single-number optimization.** Quality-Diversity research finds value in diverse behavior repertoires, rather than optimizing one task metric. For Combat Lab, this is a methodological warning: measuring distance traveled, number of contacts or box displacement cannot substitute for the diversity of interesting *actions*. **Do not** install MAP-Elites, an evolution engine, or a universal behavioral archive merely on this analogy:
- Pugh et al., *Quality Diversity: A New Frontier for Evolutionary Computation*, 2016: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2016.00040/full

The transfer here is conceptual. No external source qualifies our runtime or specifies its architecture.

## 2. Four competing explanations for the missing experience

| Hypothesis | What is missing in current specimens | A distinct whole-world possibility it should create | Hardest falsifier | Overfitting trap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **H-MATERIAL / relational substrate** | Too few kinds of enduring matter and constraint; crates + gates do most of the work | A body or object changes what *other* bodies can later do by modifying the physical arrangement, not by flipping a mission flag | Richer matter introduces no new spontaneous interventions; the same events occur if it is simplified back to crates/walls | A complicated hand-made puzzle with one intended path |
| **H-ANATOMY / physical effectors** | Body envelope and joints do not yet support broad, recombinable force and leverage | Different physical members support pushing, trapping, sweeping, holding a barrier, yielding, bracing, hooking or releasing in the *same* world | A rigid-body surrogate with the same mass, envelope and root drive produces essentially the same meaningful afterstates | Polishing the two-jaw Pincer into a one-purpose manipulation product |
| **H-COUPLING / local sensorimotor continuity** | Others react little or respond with simple drift/reverse, so events mainly originate with authored initial conditions and operator | Non-selected bodies meaningfully alter their next action because of actual sensed pressure, support, effort or obstruction, and thereby alter later matter/body possibilities | Turning this local law off changes only motion quantity or generic jams; the apparent life is wandering/traffic | Rebuilding R0/L0 as prettier physical crowd navigation or importing whole ReflexBrain |
| **H-WORKBENCH / accessible possibility discovery** | Direct editing, control, afterstate readability and unusual experimentation still carry friction | An uncoached researcher spontaneously discovers *several* different interactions and can meaningfully challenge them | Same underlying world produces no additional actual questions/actions after UI improvements; more tools only add a sidebar | Instrumentation-first product, parameter panel, badges, scores |

**None is independent in nature.** A live world could fail because an inadequate *combination* of body, matter and response blocks an otherwise plausible event. Conversely, optimizing only H-COUPLING may create pseudo-life in an empty substrate. Do not select a winner simply because it is cheapest to implement or most legible to the test harness.

## 3. Three serious whole-specimen alternatives — competing, not additive feature lists

### A. Material Commons (world-first)
A single freely editable place whose material arrangements have diverse lawful consequences: uneven/resistive surface regions, freely moving masses, real pivoted members, obstruction/contact chains and reusable physical constraints. Several body shapes need only simple finite control. User can create different situations in the *same running world*. The interesting thing is how matter and geometry mediate what different bodies can do.

**Distinct outcome:** a moved load enables, blocks or changes a later interaction for a different body, without scripted keys, portals or goals. **Kill if:** the world becomes an object-arrangement puzzle and bodies remain interchangeable. **Pro:** changes world afterstate. **Con:** may be visually inert; without body capacity diversity it replays PR #1's arrangement problem.

### B. Open Body Mechanics (anatomy-first)
One coherent physical environment featuring a *family of recombinable actual contact effectors* with different envelopes, leverage, supports and jointed movement authority. A short direct authoring loop must expose changes to physical collider/mass/inertia, not fake visuals. Multi-part bodies may move oddly or fail; that is evidence.

**Distinct outcome:** a broad body can hold/barricade something, a narrow articulated one can turn/dislodge/manipulate it, another can exploit the changed world, with alternatives rather than one mandatory role. **Kill if:** most observed success depends on executing a taught claw manipulation sequence, or rigid proxies reproduce it. **Pro:** directly exploits strongest PR #2 donor. **Con:** risks a deep one-creature-builder tunnel and a sparse world.

### C. Reciprocal Material Field (coupling-first, *not* crowd pathfinding)
A heterogeneous physically acted-upon space, with several bodies offering materially different contact possibilities and **minimal body-local responses** grounded in their own actual physical signals. They need not run around constantly; standing, bracing, yielding, investigating contact, losing an object, pausing, reorienting or withdrawing can be real behaviors. No scripted central destination, role assignment, side-of-wall command or global objective. The operator can still possess a body and author space.

**Distinct outcome:** after a free intervention, the changed contact/matter state causes the *other* bodies to modify physical actions, leading to a second consequence that was not explicitly requested, while the same world with their responses removed behaves differently. **Kill if:** active bodies merely wander into jams, periodic wiggle is mislabeled perception, or eliminating the local response changes only raw contact count. **Pro:** most direct challenge to the current cross-branch gap. **Con:** very easy to claim “life” from scripted output; may be fundamentally bottlenecked by insufficient body/world affordances.

**Working synthesis, not selected architecture:** C is worth confronting first because it targets the missing source of **consequentially self-generated events**, but only if paired with enough H-ANATOMY and H-MATERIAL to prevent a “local brain driving circles” reprise. Inverting the causal design is equally plausible: start with A or B and see whether agency naturally becomes the actual limiting factor. **Do not create PR #3 as an automatic merger or freeze C as an engineering plan.**

## 4. A discriminating prospective evidence strategy

Rather than another retrospective list of 30 machine PASSes, ask a **small number of counterfactual questions within a coherent whole**:

1. **Same matter + same external disturbance, different physical body:** is the *kind* of possible material consequence different, not just displacement magnitude? Compare actual multi-part anatomy with a rigid proxy of matching total mass/envelope and similar root authority.
2. **Same physical bodies and world, local reaction active vs locally quiet:** does actor-originated action after an observed event generate a materially different *second* event, or merely drift/traffic? Both paths must preserve the same solver and world authored truth; no target-coordinate steering.
3. **Same bodies and controls, replace heterogeneous material constraint with simple obstacle:** do meaningful new affordances disappear? If not, the supposedly rich substrate may be ornamental.
4. **Free Owner intervention instead of staged sequence:** after an arbitrary push/repose/scale/part edit, does the world remain a playground where new consequences can be learned without following a test prompt? Internal automated probes *cannot* settle this experiential question.
5. **Stress, weird regimes and safety:** deliberately combine awkward asymmetrical body forms, masses, joint strengths, density, wedging and off-axis loading. Preserve observable failures instead of clamping everything for a green dashboard, except when needed to protect runtime.
6. **Source of each event:** distinguish manually authored setup, operator-imposed impulse, actor-local motor/sensor response and passive solver dynamics. This is a research attribution lens, not a request for a permanent event-sourcing architecture. Previous PR #2 “active crowd” was largely 13 external impulses per case; never market that as autonomous ecology.

Crucial: run a no-rich-feature control whose baseline has a chance to do the same thing. Otherwise “new verb” can be ordinary collision or displacement already available with a rigid body. An A/B that changes both body and local brain cannot attribute the difference cleanly.

These counterfactuals establish only scoped physical possibilities. **Owner qualification** still requires a compelling, first-run discoverable whole; a human's negative experience cannot be erased by machine claims. Asking for Owner rehearsal before that whole exists risks repeating the historical failure.

## 5. Qualitative crossing — what would actually make this important?

A serious next whole is *candidate-worthy* if its freely editable world can support several clearly unlike, materially meaningful and comprehensible continuations **without** central role choreography. Examples of the kinds of phenomena, **not scripted objectives**: a moving obstruction becomes a shield/shelter, a narrow appendage frees a jammed mechanism that a bulky body could only push, a persistent displacement changes what a third body can do, or local pressure creates bracing/yielding that alters future access. These must arise from physics and body-specific capacities, not hidden game triggers.

A useful human-scale criterion is: someone entering the world should **notice at least a few plausible things they want to try spontaneously** and be able to disturb the system into qualitatively different responses. This is not a numerical pass threshold (“three verbs = PASS”) or a demand for self-moving NPCs everywhere. The value is the reason the experimenter asks a second question, not a test badge.

If the whole still looks like rigid traffic and box pushing after a substantial coherent construction campaign, **abandon or radically redirect the specimen**, preserving only justified donors. Do not prolong it for another cycle of UI tuning and local bug/PASS reports.

## 6. Immediate route for the next work session — permission and stop boundaries

- **Continue research and independent alternatives first.** The key newly raised issue is relational/compositional *action possibilities*, not lack of a specific AI model. Critically consider physical-world richness and open anatomy *alongside* minimal body-local reaction.
- **Before opening a branch**, choose a believable first-run physical situation supporting varied actions in a *single mutable place*, and specify the clean controls that would falsify the novelty. The situation must permit user interference, not enforce a route. Scrutinize whether the 2D top-down Rapier substrate can faithfully represent the proposed mechanics; if it cannot, change the question's embodiment or re-evaluate engine/dimension.
- **Build one substantial cohesive specimen when concept is strong enough**; internal probes only for actual uncertain mechanisms. No M1/R1/D1-style roadmap or PR #1→#2 inheritance.
- **Pause for higher-level Owner review** at a real qualitative crossroads. Public rehearsal remains separate; a green draft never becomes the deployed experience without explicit deliberate action and suitable readiness.
- **Avoid permanent meta-infrastructure:** no obligatory ontology of verbs, MAP-Elites engine, scorecard, generated "richness %" or compulsory weekly report. A little causal tracing may distinguish actor-generated effects from externally imposed ones, but the live experiment is the product.

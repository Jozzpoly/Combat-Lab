# Failure Scar — 2026-10-06

This file preserves the failure **without preserving the failed prototypes as active authority**.

## What failed

Combat Lab repeatedly used deliberately crude, short-lived fixtures to answer early questions.

The Owner explicitly warned more than once that agents tend to become attached to the first working prosthesis, defend it, elaborate it and eventually treat it as the project's sacred center even after the Owner asks to move far beyond it.

The project repeated exactly that failure.

After a long refoundation/research campaign, the Owner was shown an experience still qualitatively close to the early crude prototypes. Large amounts of local mechanistic work and green qualification had not been converted into the substantially more serious organism the Owner had repeatedly requested.

Living Movement L0 made the failure visible again: old crossing-like behavior, crude jams, restricted experimental freedom and weak Owner-facing causal access were presented after weeks of work as though local internal improvements constituted the promised qualitative advance.

## Project-level verdict

This was not merely a bad tuning pass. It was a failure to respect the lifecycle of experiments.

Temporary prostheses acquired continuity and authority they had never earned. Sunk cost, accumulated tests and local findings became reasons to preserve the wrong object.

## Corrective action

The active project was reset to the last neutral execution substrate from before the later specimen lineage.

Old research is forensic history only. It must not be recreated as authority by calling old mechanisms “qualified foundations”, “valuable because already built”, “the safest continuation”, or “too much work to throw away”.

If an old idea is genuinely useful later, it must win again against the current question.

## Permanent scar

> **A Lab that cannot throw away its experiments cannot discover anything beyond its first prototype.**

The purpose of this record is to preserve that lesson, not the implementation lineage.

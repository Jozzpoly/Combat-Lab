## 3. What the handoff got right — and what this campaign sharpens

The closure handoff correctly separates shared Lab debt from Ecology-specific organism/contact/navigation debt.

The deeper finding is that the shared problem is not merely Inspector layout.

The current shell structurally assumes:

`experiment = flat editable numeric IDs + global actions + global live values + capture-all parameter A/B + one global Debug boolean`.

That contract has reached its research limit.

Therefore refoundation must challenge the **research-state and interaction contract** before selecting tabs, docks, panels, windows or another visual layout.

A second sharpening concerns comparison:

> generic capture-all A/B is not necessarily a shared Lab primitive.

The shared apparatus may provide provenance, checkpoints and state-slice mechanisms, while each experiment defines what is legitimately comparable, what requires a matched start and which apparatus/world/specimen domains belong in a comparison.

A third sharpening concerns Debug:

> observability is a query/probe problem, not a global on/off rendering mode.

The shared Lab may provide neutral probe plumbing and observer-cost accounting. Meanings such as route, crowd pressure, contact, intent or stuck state remain experiment/substrate semantics.

## 4. Proposed responsibility boundaries — hypothesis, not frozen architecture

### Shared Lab kernel

Candidate shared responsibilities:

- experiment lifecycle / replaceability;
- input ownership and focus isolation;
- simulation / render / intervention clock truth;
- exact build and deployment provenance;
- explicit intervention/event provenance;
- neutral target/scope/domain identity where evidence proves it useful;
- checkpoint/state-slice plumbing where it can remain semantics-neutral;
- observer-cost and performance phase accounting.

The shared kernel must not learn what a resident, route, crowd, weapon, body or contact law means merely for convenience.

### Experiment-owned research surface

An experiment should be free to expose:

- its own authoring tools;
- its own intervention vocabulary;
- its own probes/diagnostics;
- its own comparison domains;
- its own reset/checkpoint semantics;
- custom high-bandwidth controls when a generic numeric control is not sufficient.

A generic schema may remain a convenience donor. It must not be the only language an experiment can speak.

### Phenomenon substrate

Ecology-specific and replaceable:

- intent/stimulus generation;
- static and dynamic feasibility;
- immediate movement selection;
- stuck/progress semantics;
- contact/yield/displacement law;
- broad phase / neighborhood bookkeeping;
- population/cohort behavior;
- local crowd metrics.

### Observation/evidence surface

The world remains primary.

Detailed evidence should become selective, query-driven and scale-aware rather than cloning/rendering every actor's full diagnostic state every frame.

The apparatus must be able to answer causal questions without becoming a dominant runtime load.

## 5. Ecology causal decomposition

Future Ecology work must keep these concepts separable enough to diagnose:

1. **intent** — why/where an actor currently wants to act or move;
2. **feasibility/navigation** — which motions/routes are physically possible;
3. **immediate motion choice** — what the actor chooses now from feasible options;
4. **contact outcome** — what actually happens when occupied bodies meet;
5. **factual progress** — whether the actor is getting closer to completing its current purpose.

This is a causal-separation requirement, not a demand for five classes or systems.

The competence target is deliberately a floor:

> an actor should notice persistent lack of progress, understand ordinary static/dynamic obstruction well enough to stop behaving like a blind particle, and alter a failed immediate plan — without a hidden crowd policy choreographing the desired experimental answer.

## 6. Contact invariant

Owner feedback that bodies block too much does **not** authorize removal of physical occupancy.

The campaign rejects both easy extremes:

- accidental hard-disc crystallization as if it were meaningful embodied blocking;
- ghosting / hidden collision disablement / automatic despawn as a flow fix.

The research question is the middle regime: when bodies yield, resist, displace, hold space, redirect or remain genuinely blocked, and how envelope, mass, locomotor authority, posture or later mechanics contribute.

No contact solver or physics backend is selected yet.

## 8. Bounded experiment ladder

| ID | Question | Minimum discriminator | Falsifier / stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L0 — Intervention provenance cell** | Can live authoring remain fast while every consequential edit/action gains explicit scope, target and time provenance? | After an exploratory sequence, the causal intervention sequence can be reconstructed without remembering panel state. | Provenance materially slows ordinary authoring, requires a rigid domain ontology, or still cannot distinguish apparatus/specimen/world effects. |
| **L1 — Scoped comparison cell** | Can comparison be experiment-defined rather than capture-all parameter state? | A comparison visibly names its captured domains and matched-start assumptions; applying it cannot silently alter unrelated apparatus/world state. | Hidden state still changes, or every experiment is forced back into one global numeric snapshot model. |
| **O0 — Causal observation cell** | Can the Lab answer a local question such as “why is this actor stuck?” without global debug spaghetti? | A selected actor/region exposes intent → feasibility → immediate motion → contact/progress evidence with bounded observer cost. | Explanation still requires all-actor overlays, or observation cost becomes comparable to the phenomenon being measured. |
| **N0 — Whole-body feasibility cell** | Can a body reason about static traversability without adopting a crowd-behavior policy? | Different envelopes produce truthful route/clearance outcomes through the same geometry; hard feasibility remains distinct from optional comfort. | Endpoint probes still lie, or preference padding becomes indistinguishable from physical impossibility. |
| **N1 — Minimal progress/replan cell** | What is the smallest competence that prevents blind persistence after ordinary obstruction? | A resident detects persistent no-progress and changes a failed immediate plan within a bounded window. | Success depends on random wandering, reciprocal crowd choreography, ghosting, or a policy that encodes the intended solution. |
| **C0 — Contact semantics cell** | Which occupied-body relations preserve physicality without accidental crystallization? | Controlled conflicting motion intents produce legible yield/resist/displace/hold outcomes whose causes survive changes in mass/envelope/motor authority. | Solver order/instability dominates the result, or flow is achieved only by deleting physical consequence. |
| **P0 — Scaling attribution cell** | Where does runtime cost actually move after richer competence/contact exists? | Cost can be attributed separately to simulation/contact/navigation/render/authoring/observation phases while population is pushed until break. | The apparatus itself dominates cost or the Lab reports only one context-free “lag” signal. |
| **E1 — Integrated ecology reattempt** | Do minimally competent embodied actors produce useful spatial ecology across sparse → crowd → break pressure without harness-authored convergence? | Body/authority differences materially change choices and outcomes in a scenario whose stimulus topology is explicit and not secretly prescribing the answer. | Apparatus/harness artifacts again dominate before the target hypothesis can be interpreted. |

The ladder is intentionally not a promise to implement every cell. Each result can collapse, reorder or eliminate later work.

## 9. Execution order

`L0 contract/provenance design → L1/O0 minimal research apparatus → N0 static feasibility → N1 progress/replan → C0 contact cells → P0 measured scaling → integrated Ecology only if the preceding substrate earns it`.

## 3. Claims that survive; claims that do not

**Supported narrow engineering evidence** from the integrated candidate: real Rapier rigid-body/multibody joints and active contacts; constrained reciprocal material manipulation; different body/box masses affecting specified motion outcomes; finite external-ground-reaction approximations; a prismatic two-segment internal stroke with no net free-space travel in specified zero-support controls; finite bracing affecting displacement; a matched crowd test where changing brace authority first changed the corresponding physical trajectory at the first applied brace impulse, and then some shared material afterstate; reproducible *starting-scene* recipes; a small authored-world intervention UI; narrowly tested emitted browser boot and controls.

**Not established:** convincing organisms or natural gait; flexible/soft anatomy; intuitive and diverse *spontaneously discoverable actions*; actual long-term actor initiative/learning; fun, feel, rich ecology, large-scale crowds/FPS, sustained stress robustness, full simulation replay, reliable import of those findings into the wider world. The 76-body short trial and four authored scene fixtures do not qualify long-lived, deformable crowds. Contact incidences are not unique contacts.

**Material architecture risks shown by live code and actual screenshots:**
1. `dart` and `broad` are essentially rigid compound bodies; the `crawler` uses one hinge and the `worm` one prismatic link. Their silhouettes and interactions can still resemble motorized blocks. Changing colors, masses, labels or response clocks does not alone make distinct organisms.
2. All autonomous types mostly use the same shallow `localResponse` law: nearly constant forward throttle, sinusoidal drift and contact-pressure yielding/bracing. It is actor-local, but neither cognition nor evidence of open-ended behavioral repertoire.
3. Ground traction and inchworm support are finite *authored top-down force proxies*, not solver-generated foot-ground support or empirically accepted biomechanics.
4. The four presets vary initial matter/actors around the same static world, not a broadly editable heterogeneous material substrate. Pre-arranged jams risk becoming the explanation for every interesting afterstate.
5. Visual improvements were measured in agent-inspected 1440×900 emitted Chromium frames; the physical world was still recognizably simple geometric shapes. UI correctness and better instrumentation are not substitutes for Owner-level phenomena.
6. The runtime still needs independent evidence for unusual body scales, very dense crowds, prolonged joint stability, practical user control and actual performance. Do not silently infer these from bounded numerical PASS.

There is a particularly dangerous circular comparison: `new implementation -> newly introduced defect -> defect repaired -> green test` can appear to be major progress while remaining below the Owner's older baseline.

## 4. Scope and next decision boundary — explicitly open

The independently reselected body/world question remains legitimate. **The current implementation has not earned the right to define its answer or the next architecture.** Treat it as a *replaceable experiment and possible donor*, not a foundation or canonical organism. Do not promote, merge, expand or delete it solely from sunk cost, test count, novelty labels or the last agent's `NEXT`. Preserve live availability and provenance.

The next valuable independent judgment is **phenomenon-level versus Owner-rejected baseline**: without telling the operator a scripted goal, does free disturbance of materially distinct bodies/world create several qualitatively different, understandable actions and causes rather than traffic, jams and parameter variations? Can the operator stress/extensively break the world without paternalistic caps? Is a genuinely new physical capability needed rather than a more attractive view of the existing one?

If new material evidence is absent, the current branch may be retained as a mechanical donor and its organism model rejected. If a stronger *whole* is conceived, derive the representation it needs rather than extending this branch by habit. No automatic mandatory NEXT is selected by this record.

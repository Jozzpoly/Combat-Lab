# Combat Lab — Fresh Frontier Reassessment — 2026-10-07

**Status:** frontier-selection record · no implementation architecture authority  
**Parent state:** recovery quarantine after 2026-10-06 failure  
**Purpose:** select one discovery question from broad Owner intent and surviving evidence without allowing historical implementation ancestry to choose it.

## 1. Evidence that survives the reset

The useful human evidence is broader than any single old implementation.

### Material body/world relation

S0/B0 produced strong positive Owner evidence that:

- body envelope changes reachable spatial relations;
- body mass changes body-body dynamics;
- different embodied phenotypes are perceptible enough to provoke deliberate Owner exploration;
- the body/world direction is valuable even when no weapon mechanic provides the answer.

This is the strongest repeated positive direction signal in the old project.

### Material contact / afterstate

R3 produced an early positive Owner signal around persistent material contact/afterstate.

It is a donor direction, not permission to restore sword-centric development.

### Collective embodied phenomena

R0 produced a bounded positive signal that several simple heterogeneous material bodies can create recognizable collective effects such as compression, release, queuing and residual deadlock without a central crowd coordinator.

That does not qualify the old crowd implementation, traffic topology or navigation policy.

### Whole-organism failures

Terrarium, O1/O2 and other whole-organism failures established that:

- causally correct mechanics can still produce a poor whole;
- parameter/mechanism richness is not possibility richness;
- a technically qualified implementation is not automatically worth Owner attention;
- world geometry, body relation and tools matter only when they change what can meaningfully be done.

## 2. Fresh candidate families

These are compared as research questions, not implementation roadmaps.

### A — Embodied material agency beyond transit

Question family:

> when materially different bodies share a varied, manipulable world, do those differences create several useful continuations the Owner discovers naturally, beyond fit/no-fit and crowd flow?

Potential value:

- strongest direct Owner evidence;
- high leverage across movement, tools, terrain, multiple actors, future combat and co-op;
- speaks directly to the wider material-world vision;
- can remain useful without importing cognition architecture from Companion / ReflexBrain / SPC.

Primary risk:

- collapsing again into pathfinding, pedestrian flow, one-choke puzzles or physics parameter theater.

### B — Persistent tool / weapon as body extension

Question family:

> can materially persistent tools create distinct spatial possibilities through reach, clearance, coverage, mass and contact inside the same world?

Potential value:

- R3 and older contact work contain positive seeds;
- high human discriminability;
- direct combat relevance.

Primary risk:

- re-entering the old melee/contact tunnel before the broader body/world substrate earns it.

### C — Ranged / magic spatial relation

Question family:

> can ranged or magic interaction create materially different space/world relations rather than becoming target -> projectile/damage variants?

Potential value:

- high orthogonality;
- major blind spot;
- strong future mixed-modality value.

Primary risk:

- weak direct Owner evidence today;
- easy collapse into projectile demo or colored damage before body/world relations are rich enough.

### D — Multi-actor relation / protection / screening

Question family:

> can several embodied actors create useful local relations such as screening, interception, holding, yielding and protecting without global choreography?

Potential value:

- crowd-like and screening evidence exists;
- high relevance to future co-op and living worlds.

Primary risk:

- very easy to regress into crowd AI, pathfinding, Companion behavior or authored role scripts.

### E — Living cognition / local brain

This is important to the wider dream but is **not selected as a Combat Lab frontier**.

ReflexBrain / Companion / SPC own cognition, continuity and higher-level actor intelligence more directly.

Combat Lab should provide material capability/evidence that those projects can later consume.

## 3. Selection

The strongest fresh discovery frontier is **A — embodied material agency beyond transit**.

This is not a continuation of Ecology/R0/L0.

The old code, traffic topology, route system, contact policy and crowd harness have no authority.

The selected working question is:

> **When a small number of materially different embodied actors share a varied and manipulable world, do body/world differences create multiple useful, legible possibilities that the Owner naturally discovers — beyond simple traversal, fit/no-fit and crowd flow?**

This question earns selection because:

- it has the strongest repeated human-positive evidence;
- it attacks assumptions shared by melee, ranged, magic, tools and future co-op;
- it is broad enough to escape the old crowd tunnel but narrow enough to test as one serious whole;
- it contributes material-world capability to the wider dream without turning Combat Lab into an NPC/cognition project;
- it can be falsified by ordinary Owner behavior rather than by one preferred metric.

## 4. What this frontier must not become

The next specimen fails its research purpose if it becomes primarily:

- pedestrian/crowd traffic;
- pathfinding research;
- one narrow choke / one small-body route;
- body-size classes;
- a parameter spreadsheet with circles attached;
- a central AI assigning roles;
- an instrumentation/dashboard project;
- a port of old R0/L0 mechanics;
- a chain of M1/R1/D1-style cells standing in for a whole;
- a capped safe playground that prevents deliberate overload or strange combinations.

No old mechanism is automatically required.

## 5. Serious-construction requirement

This frontier should not begin with another long chain of tiny fixtures.

The next substantial implementation should be a **coherent serious discovery specimen** built fresh enough that its whole can be judged against the actual question.

Small internal falsifiers remain allowed when a concrete technical uncertainty needs them.

They do not become the architecture by default.

The specimen must eventually contain enough world/body interaction that a negative Owner judgement would be evidence about the frontier rather than merely evidence that the prototype was empty.

## 6. Pre-implementation unknowns

Selection of the question does **not** select:

- physics engine;
- actor representation;
- pathfinding/navigation architecture;
- exact body shapes;
- exact contact law;
- carrying/object mechanics;
- tools/weapons;
- 2D vs semantic height/2.5D;
- resident intelligence;
- number of actors;
- final Workbench UI.

Those choices require separate technical research against this question.

## 7. Hard falsifiers

Reassess or stop the frontier if:

- materially different bodies still provoke essentially the same useful behavior;
- the only meaningful distinction is authored fit/no-fit geometry;
- the interesting behavior disappears when crowd density is low;
- navigation sophistication becomes the main phenomenon;
- resident policy has to know the intended role/route;
- world manipulation does not change meaningful continuations;
- the specimen remains qualitatively equivalent to R0/L0 despite internal sophistication;
- the apparatus consumes more attention than the phenomenon.

## Working invariant

> **The selected frontier is body/world possibility, not the old body/world implementation.**

# Y1 — relational composition vs real second-body actions (2026-10-10)

**Scope and verdict:** initial competing *composition-first* whole using source-tested X0 Rapier physics on a new isolated branch from neutral main `228dee4f`. **NARROW POSITIVE** for changed physical access and externally operated body-to-matter action; **QUALITATIVE WHOLE STILL UNQUALIFIED / GENERALITY WEAK**. No Owner observation. No PR/merge/public rehearsal. Historical R0/R1/L0 Owner product FAILs remain unchanged.

## Why Y1 exists

The X0 first-run world was sparse and actor-local tactile reactions, while mechanically real, produced no changed *second body* material-hold opportunity in its nine examined settings. Y1 tests a competing cause: maybe the problem is first-run **relational composition**. It uses the *same* conditional body geometry, finite force budgets, sensory reactions and material implementation, but rearranges six physical organisms and eleven mobile/constrained materials into one overlapping, editable, open physical field. No task targets, phase triggers, NPC routes, scripted victory or central brain.

The live browser was inspected at start and after simulation. Most activity remained in a subset of bodies; this is still a stylized mechanical workbench, not a living ecosystem. An incidental visual defect (large shaded fake 'areas' with no solver collider) was removed: the remaining visible solid obstacles correspond to real material. Such a visual correction is not an Owner feel PASS.

## Source and matched physical observations

A powered world-anchored hinge provides **externally authored finite energy**, NOT organism initiative. In a 360-tick paired trial with no possessed actor, this different authored composition gave 155 actor-contact-active ticks, five local reflexes and three contacted actors, versus one contact tick and no reflex with the same starting material motor disabled. The motor was explicitly ablated and the actual Rapier afterstates examined. Counts are diagnostic; contact quantity is not a new action.

**A narrow change in actor opportunity did occur:** with world energy ON, two different organisms had additional actual-near-contact material-hold eligibility at tick 360:
- Second organism (broad rigid hull, actor-10): newly eligible for `matter-15` and real `hinge-23`.
- Third organism (single articulated lever, actor-11): newly eligible for `matter-16` and `hinge-23`.
- Others: no new hold opportunity in the checked same-world pair.

Eligibility is X0's **0.18 m geometric near-touch approximation**, not a measured active tactile manifold and not an actual human grasp. We did not move objects to enable the test; all bodies and material occupied their actual evolved positions.

**Real second-stage operator action**, after switching all external powered hinges OFF: actor-11 held free `matter-16` and was manually driven left with finite root force. Matched output:
- Hold + manual movement: object ΔX = **−2.9563 m**.
- No hold + same manual movement: **−0.5993 m**.
- Hold + body idle: **−0.3914 m**.
- No earlier world energy: hold unavailable, object did not move under the same commanded actor movement.

This rejects a specific **residual-motion-only** explanation of the second-stage displacement. It proves an externally operated conditional action, **not a body choosing to do it autonomously**. The comparison is **exploratory and post-hoc**, chosen after inspecting the original Y1 world outcome, not a preregistered universal success rate.

## Rivals, weaker cases and falsifiers

**Near-neighborhood variation:** at actor-11 initial vertical offsets −0.25, 0, +0.25 m, prior world power still enabled its later finite hold on free matter when the passive-world counterpart could not; held-with-movement effects exceeded the same commanded unheld action by **2.357–2.5325 m** in magnitude. This is local robustness only.

**Wider tests were not uniformly positive:**
- At **−1 m**, neither world allowed the hold; no extra grip or contact-only effect.
- At **−0.5 m**, energized world allowed hold, passive did not; held action added 2.8306 m (X magnitude) over unheld; contact-only movement added about 0.1973 m with just one directly measured target-contact tick.
- At **0 m**, same distinction held.
- At **+0.5 m**, neither allowed the hold and there was no gained action.
- At **+1 m**, both worlds allowed hold; the *supposedly new* action was not source-specific. Here physical contact-only pushing was already available and dominated, with 100 direct contact ticks; do not count it as world-power-enabled capability.

**Without adhesive hold:** the actor could still influence the free material through actual solver contacts: in the earlier ±0.25m neighborhood the manually moving/no-grip body produced an additional ΔX of ~0.1945–0.3214m over the same body idle. **7–21 solver-active direct body→target contact ticks** were verified in those trials, against fewer and weaker idle contacts. This is small but real *non-grip* material influence.

**Anchored hinge alternative:** the broad rigid actor could acquire a hold on an actual pinned beam only in the powered stage-1 field. Its later controlled operation rotated the hinge by ~**−0.3536 rad** versus **−0.1130 rad** without the grip, while the finite holding link eventually slipped. However: **the no-grip moving trial showed zero solver-active direct body→hinge contacts and zero additional rotation compared with no-grip idle**. So the 'different hinge action' in this setup is mediated by the same powered adhesive link, not independent physical leverage. This is a central negative counterexample.

## Adjudication

- **What improved over X0:** a scene with richer contact coupling can *sometimes* alter a second real body's physically available later action in the same world, while keeping body-local controls unchanged. Different material constraints can respond differently to a later operator-applied bodily force.
- **What did NOT improve enough:** generalized body-action diversity, resident self-initiation, natural gripping/contact, readable first-run experimentation or broadly robust second-body actions. Most measured dramatic effects remain dependent on geometric positioning and on the same temporary adhesive hold. Y1 still lacks any Owner-experiential judgement.
- **No feature nextness:** do not 'fix' the negative controls by iteratively tuning coordinates, rewriting thresholds or adding more powered hinges. Preserve the donor/observation and return to the larger body↔world possibility question. A subsequent serious whole should consider contact-only body methods, physical screening/pushing/displacement, richer material topology and representation honestly, with matched simple rigid/adhesion-free alternatives. This is a **research direction**, not a frozen architecture or mandatory plan.

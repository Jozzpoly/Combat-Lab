# Combat Lab — independent whole-frontier reselection after X0 falsifiers (2026-10-10)

**Status:** working research decision, not architecture, implementation roadmap, Owner approval, PR merge or human-quality PASS. Latest [RESEARCH_STATE](RESEARCH_STATE.md) governs. The historical Owner R0/R1/L0 failures remain product-level failures. X0 remains an isolated replaceable donor.

## What actually changed our belief

1. **H-COUPLING survived a narrow causal test.** A real local tactile limb reflex, on/off in nine prespecified whole-field variations, changed later physical material afterstates in 8/9. In some cases removing only the first body's reflex altered the *timing* of the second body's otherwise intact reflex, but not its number or type of event.
2. **Action creation did not follow.** At the end of those same nine matched worlds, the second organism could acquire **no** material through the existing near-touch body-hold action in either world. This cannot disprove pushing or other actions, but it is a serious counterexample to celebrating motion divergence as expanded second-body possibilities. An earlier "second actor moved material" claim had already failed an idle-actor control.
3. **H-ANATOMY has only a narrow positive.** Physically rebuilding a 0-arm organism to 2 arms changed what it could reach, and independent motorized arm sweeps can displace material with root drive disabled. Yet those experiments did not pit the action against a rigid surrogate with matched envelope/mass and similar controllable motion. Two-hinged-arm morphology is a restricted mechanism vocabulary, not an open organism.
4. **H-MATERIAL remains underspecified.** A powered constrained beam can be blocked by real geometry and provoke localized contact/arm bracing. This is sourced world energy, not creature initiative. Agent-inspected real first-frame and 6-second Chromium scenes still appear sparse and do not naturally offer several interrelated actions.
5. **H-WORKBENCH is mechanically improving, not experientially qualified.** The edit/observe/rebuild loop exists, but there has been no human demonstration that it elicits novel questions. Sidebar controls and a large mostly empty field are likely barriers, not solved by more diagnostics.

These observations are *reasons to confront a different whole*, not votes from a numeric feature scoreboard. They do not establish that X0 is impossible or intrinsically bad; they establish insufficient evidence to keep promoting its architecture.

## The next independent working bet

**Body↔material possibility density, not more reflex complexity.** Explore a coherent single editable place where *qualitatively different bodily actions* are possible because physical members, constraints and material relationships overlap meaningfully. Examples of research pressure, NOT mandated gameplay verbs or one designed solution: resist/brace a member, displace a free load, manipulate a fixed pivot, be prevented from acting by a physical envelope, and have another body subsequently exploit or fail to exploit the resulting changed world. Physics must adjudicate outcomes, not hidden quest flags.

Three rival explanations must remain live:

- **Richer material relations first**: if same bodies gain several new kinds of action after real object rearrangement, world substrate was limiting. Falsify against simple free blocks/fixed obstacles.
- **Richer force-bearing body first**: if matched rigid hulls cannot reproduce expressive limb/support action, anatomy was limiting. Falsify with comparable mass, envelope and drive, not a tiny hull handicapped against a long arm.
- **Neither helps until input/perception is better**: if an otherwise plausible whole still gives the Owner no natural experimentation loop, focus on embodied control/readability instead of a third servo. This is not a license for an instrumentation project.

**Do not freeze any winner on paper.** A full separate candidate can reuse source-tested X0 mechanics, but its actual whole, physical substrate, action language and scene composition must be independently justified. Another chain of successful isolated test fixtures is not a substitute for this candidate.

## Concrete standard for the next serious run

A prospective whole should make an observer want to try **several physically different interventions** without instruction, with the same continuous world allowing them to alter one another. Build enough density and relation for actual choices, not random clutter/crowd wandering or a locked "puzzle solution." Leave bodies quiet where no material cue or purpose exists, and do not install a global target/path brain.

Before Owner exposure, the agent must verify that (a) a real second body has at least one *changed actionable relationship*, with later action input isolated from residual momentum; (b) any claimed reflex-mediated relation changes only after own-sensed input and finite effort; (c) simple rigid surrogate and passive-material alternatives get a fair chance; (d) no obvious edit/control/runtime defects dominate. This is a **prospective falsification plan**, not machine certification of fun.

If a substantially different coherent whole still invites only watching collisions, admit that the current 2D, traction-proxy, cuboid-limb representation might be inadequate or that physical behavior alone is insufficient. Retain donor evidence, reconsider representation and do not drag X0 forward by sunk cost.

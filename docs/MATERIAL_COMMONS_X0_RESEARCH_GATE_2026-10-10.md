# Combat Lab — Material Commons X0: a falsifiable whole, not a selected architecture

**2026-10-10 · RESEARCH CANDIDATE / PROVISIONAL BEST NEXT DISCRIMINATOR**  
**Owner qualification: NONE. Implementation: NOT STARTED.**  
This is a bounded experiment *proposal*, not a new canonical foundation, a feature checklist, a committed runtime, an Owner request to merge, or approval of either previous draft. The default route remains live [RESEARCH_STATE](RESEARCH_STATE.md) and most recent explicit Owner corrections. This is an outcome of [competing whole-world hypotheses](COMPETING_WHOLE_WORLD_HYPOTHESES_2026-10-10.md).

**Physical mechanism preflight finished (bounded, not X0):** [observations and failed cases](X0_LOCAL_MATERIAL_CHAIN_PREFLIGHT_2026-10-10.md) show actual touch-driven arm actuation causing a later crate-to-crate contact afterstate difference in **one trajectory-informed setup**. No second actor responded; three other mass/impulse combinations yielded smaller, absent or contact-free continuations. This supports only narrow feasibility and strengthens the need for a *whole-world* experiment where qualitative actions can change for another body. Do not keep tuning that fixture or claim X0 has begun.

## Executive decision — the missing causal crossing

The earlier work established real, but *separate*, facts:
- PR #1 can couple simple actor-local pressure reactions to the physical world, but its policy maintains nearly constant propulsion, a small wander sinusoid, and give-way/recover. It tends toward R0/L0-like transit. Body shapes largely remain motorized geometry.
- PR #2 can use two independently actuated, collision-bearing limbs to alter material motion, including without artificial grip attachment. However `step` applies the user's movement input only to the selected actor; non-selected actors hold their posture/brake and remain mostly passive. Density tests produce effects largely from authored initial collisions, outside impulses and research-scripted aperture changes.

These are **two useful donors, not two halves of a ready-made system**.

**Choose to research the *conditional material consequence*, not “more roaming AI”, “more claws” or “more interesting crates”.** The next decisive difference is:

> A physical disturbance changes the **material circumstances of one body**; its own finite local reaction changes **another physical relation**; the changed world then alters a **second actor's available action**. None of the later consequences is a goal/role/route scripted specifically for this setup.

This is a *prospective discriminator*, not a claim that a future runtime will do it or that a longer causal chain automatically feels alive. Manual first disturbance is permitted and honestly attributed to the researcher.

### Working name

**Material Commons X0** — a *single* editable shared material space in which heterogeneous bodies and constraint-bearing matter cause each other to gain, lose or change possible physical actions. The word `Commons` signifies one shared world/afterstate, not multiple mini-level puzzles. `X0` is a research label, not a permanent model of future modes.

## One physical place; no authored puzzle route

Begin with a modest continuous field that visibly contains, in no prescribed order:
- open space for arbitrary construction and collisions, **not** a mandatory chokepoint;
- free bodies of noticeably different real mass/envelopes;
- one or two articulated bodies with physically different contact/reach capacities;
- several freely repositionable masses, rods/plates and at least one world-pinned hinged member whose contacts can redirect force;
- a few *locally constrained material relationships*: an anchored bar, a load in contact, a loose object near a changeable contact path. **Their arrangement is a starting condition only.** No target object, hidden win state, left/right routing logic, rewards, doors requiring “key body X”, role assignments, fake scripted material tags or out-of-range auto-reset.

An Owner entering should immediately see more than stationary colored blocks: actual joint/part movement where the body has physical actuation, existing motion where it has a physical cause, and clear contact consequences. Avoid choreographing a “watch it solve the obstacle” performance. Stillness can be meaningful when a body is actively holding against a force; constant roaming is not mandatory.

The strongest *potential* moment is not a staged success but a subsequent unexpected change after a free interference, e.g. moving a heavy slab alters the load on a hinged barrier; a body adjusts physical pressure/arm posture because that load changed; another body can now physically push, pass or fail differently. A rigid surrogate or a local-response-off control must be able to **falsify** any claimed novelty.

The world is **reconfigurable live**: pause/reposition/rotate, alter mass/envelope/reach/finite actuator authority, spawn diverse bodies, vary material load, resume, keep consequences. Scene capture stores a *starting condition*, not false running solver-state replay. If a seemingly compelling effect cannot survive Owner rearranging the environment, it is only an authored fixture and must be demoted.

## Actor-local policy: a purposely small opposite of R0 wandering

The first local response should work from **owned physical observations** only: actual contact normal/load and its location on a body or arm, relative motion/force against a pose, the body’s own motor effort, body orientation, own short contact history, and whether its finite actuator reached a limit. No world-global desired target, magic identity of a scripted puzzle object, global path, crossing objective, knowledge of the researcher’s test, or external event that flips a pre-authored narrative phase.

Candidate reactions are physically grounded **regulations** rather than “NPC personalities”:
- resist physically applied displacement up to a finite available motor/brace budget; stronger loads win;
- allow a loaded arm to yield or release rather than clamp infinitely;
- adapt another limb's finite force after genuine load imbalance, if that relation is physically observable;
- stop working against an impossible configuration instead of driving into a jam forever;
- choose not to move when there is no locally meaningful input. Different initial configuration/response parameters can lead to different outcomes. They are **hypotheses**, not behavior commitments.

Especially important: **no unconditional `throttle=0.85` shared wandering loop** masquerading as self-initiated physical life. Conversely, merely holding a servo setpoint is still a mechanism, not cognitive agency. A sensor-driven continuation that materially alters another body's situation has higher scientific value.

The researcher retains direct possession/control of an organism and an *explicit external disturbance tool*, distinguishable from organism-originated actions. Quiet actors are allowed. A mixed autonomous/motorized crowd remains an **open** later question; do not claim it solved.

## Four clean falsifiers of X0

**F1 — Does another body gain or lose an action?** Keep identical initial geometry/mass/contacts and a single finite external disturbance. Compare a body with physical effector authority to a rigid approximation with closely matched mass/envelope/root actuation. Look for **different kinds of continuation** (constraint released, new approach becomes possible, changed holding/blocked relation), not simply bigger distance/contact count. If all differences are explained by a longer hull or stronger root motor, reject the “new articulated affordance” claim.

**F2 — Is local response causally necessary for the *second* event?** Use the same true bodies and initial material state, with only one body-local response channel ablated. The initial perturbation is identical; the meaningful divergence must occur at or after actual **contact/sensed load** and traced to a physically delivered finite actuator command. If divergence precedes sensing, or only total movement increases, reject the “causal local loop” claim. Avoid global afterstate “magic” and do not label the researcher’s input an autonomous event.

**F3 — Does richer material matter?** Use the same actor/body response and source disturbance, but remove a hinged or coupled constraint while preserving as much of mass/envelope as meaningful. Does the set of possible physical continuations materially change? If the scene works just as well with crates and fixed walls, the richer material representation was decoration. Do not add geometry just to make F3 green.

**F4 — Can an uncoached experimenter discover and break it?** Without an externally supplied puzzle task, observe whether first-frame shape/contacts and direct controls invite *more than one* plausible physical experiment, and whether disturbing one mechanism produces comprehensible changes. Only actual Owner observation may qualify feel, usefulness or delight. Agent-rendered screenshots and emitted-browser tests can identify UI failures, never substitute for Owner PASS. If this world still feels like one improved traffic/jam toy, **stop and reconsider the body/world representation** rather than polishing more metrics.

Additional falsifiers: substantially more bodies; extreme masses; off-axis impulse on a real limb; contact-coupled hinge near singular configurations; temporary loss of support; changing actuators mid-experiment; deliberately awkward geometry; timer/false FPS measurement elimination. Failures must remain visible and reproducible, not erased by invisible teleport or per-scene special cases.

## Evidence provenance — avoid the previous measurement illusion

Every interesting chain should be attributable (in lightweight, optional diagnostics, not a permanent event bus):
`author set initial condition` → `researcher applied finite stimulus` → `Rapier solved actual contacts` → `body-local observation updated` → `actuator applied finite force/torque` → `later physical constraint/contact changed`.

Labels of **source** must be distinct: authoring, external experimenter impulse, actor-local response, passive solver. The same event can have multiple necessary causes. A simulation screenshot alone cannot prove who initiated a behavior; initial crowd contact can be caused by placement, not local agency. Show actual before/after and a matched counterfactual only when qualifying a mechanistic claim.

No “15 new verbs” scoreboard, global emergence index, magic discrete “lever unlocked” trigger or synthetic success counter. If a material relation creates new optional actions, describe it through its physical cause. Use targeted instrumentation only where confidence would otherwise be fake.

## Representation and implementation alternatives

**2D planar Rapier is a provisional tool for contact/leverage/topology, not a fidelity claim for biomechanics.** Top-down `gravity=(0,0)` has no actual vertical foot support; present-day motor/traction and bracing forces are explicitly finite *external ground proxies*. No gait/terrain deformation/real normal-load claim may follow from it. If the phenomena that matter depend on stepping, shifting vertical load or a body lying on terrain, revisit dimensional representation before building complex compensatory hacks.

**Joint physics fork is unresolved:** PR #2 uses Rapier impulse joints; dense-load anchor drift reached centimeters. Official JavaScript API advertises `World.createMultibodyJoint`, whose reduced-coordinate tree constraints could be better for particular limb chains, but may sacrifice arbitrary graph/loop topology, editing flexibility and performance. **Investigate the exact pinned `@dimforge/rapier2d-deterministic@0.21.0` package**, not only latest web API, with one narrow representative joint chain before freezing morphology. Retain impulse joints for closed loops or dynamic authoring if the evidence favors them. This is a *technical risk gate*, not the selected architecture. See https://rapier.rs/javascript2d/classes/World.html and https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/templates/joint_constraints/.

**No PR #1 + PR #2 merge by default.** The candidate may start from neutral main, borrow isolated source-tested mathematical/physics donor ideas, or use a different architecture if needed. Existing units must pass new whole-world falsifiers, not gain authority through provenance.

## Scale of a first *real* candidate

The first serious implementation, if selected after falsification design, should be a **coherent whole** with:
- one shared persistent and editable world, not separate mechanistic demo worlds;
- at least two materially unlike bodies with distinct physical contact/action capacities (not only mass/color);
- one constrained/movable material relationship with persistent consequences;
- one small genuine local response based on actual physical input, with an ablation off switch for research;
- direct Owner control and intervention, immediate edit/resume loop, and readable physical silhouettes;
- one *observed* causal chain from disturbance to local response to later changed possibility, with negative controls.

These are **minimum ingredients for asking a meaningful question**, not an evaluation score or promised features. Do not make a particular humanoid/crab/claw anatomy, material type, NPC policy or control mapping canonical. Prefer a model that supports wide morphology variations, but do not build a full rig editor before observing qualitative value.

### Independent preflight decision

Before implementation: on paper and in the exact runtime if useful, try to disprove that a simple contact-responsive servo can create the intended **second material consequence** without hidden global knowledge. Analyze whether two bodies and one free constrained object can physically participate in that loop under the current 2D representation. If no convincing causal mechanism is plausible, **DO NOT start PR #3**: choose a richer material topology, improve body actuator authority, or change the question. A general simulation cannot force an outcome merely by listing desired emergent verbs.

If the mechanism is credible, build a fresh isolated experimental candidate and inspect actual browser imagery/controls early. Stop before Owner rehearsal when the whole would still teach only “boxes can push,” not a new embodied possibility.

## Stop conditions and Owner checkpoint

Pause and involve Owner at a meaningful whole-world transition, not an arbitrary number of commits: when (a) there are physically demonstrated *different* body/world continuations, (b) the entire first-run interaction can be experienced and challenged, and (c) negative cases and obvious mechanical debts are visible rather than hidden. Earlier Owner FAILs remain product-level FAILs for those older specimens; X0 currently has **no Owner-experience status**.

If the candidate makes no new qualitative action possible, preserve donor science and reject its implementation. Do not let this memo command continuation by inertia.

## Why

Previous Z1 contact-only chain established an operator-origin second-body contact after an earlier pushed object in **three author-chosen positions**. However, it did not ask whether the second body's two articulated arms were needed, or whether `secondMoves=false` meant *all* second-body effectors had stopped. Neither was previously demonstrated.

**T0 directly attacks those omissions without making another visual fixture.**

## Method / attribution

- Five predeclared second-body placements: `(17.1,11.1)`, `(18.0,11.1)`, `(18.9,11.1)` (historical) plus `(18,10.2)`, `(18,12.0)` (hostile off-axis).
- Three phase-2 morphologies: original jointed reach body, arm-free **same root footprint** with approximate **113 kg total mass** and same root motor/speed, and generously enlarged arm-free rigid envelope (NOT an exact matching surface/mass distribution).
- The same jointed second organism participates through stage 1 in every anatomical comparison. Stage 1: chosen first body rightward push ON/OFF for 180 fixed solver steps, 40-step settle. **Only then** editor-driven physical re-rig of second organism *in the same world*, preserving the object afterstate/time. Matched control worlds repeat the same full sequence.
- Stage 2: second root move left ON/OFF over 120 steps. Two *additional* phase-2 controls disable motor, joint torque and brace, testing actual unpowered behavior rather than only zero requested root movement.
- 5 placements × 3 variants × (four root-drive counterfactuals + two genuinely passive checks) = **90 separately instantiated, source-equivalent physical trial worlds**, 340 steps each. No adhesive hold, no powered material motor. This is NOT one uninterrupted 90-trial world, and is not a natural population.
- The harness rejects altered object position/clock at the instant of physical phase-2 re-rig and checks stage-1 object X across anatomical variants. A matched pair's own momentum, other-body indirect effects and other physical contacts may still exist; outcomes are not isolated to only the measured direct pair.

## Off-axis counterfactual and idle-meaning correction

- At `(18,12)`: jointed has only **2** direct contact ticks under root input; same-root arm-free body **0**. That is too small and staged to qualify meaningful articulated superiority. At `(18,10.2)`: both have **0** direct contact ticks under moving root input.
- **New crucial attribution correction:** at `(18,10.2)`, original jointed `secondMoves=false` still registers **13 direct contact ticks**, but zero-motor/zero-joint-torque/zero-brace control registers **0**. Source `CommonsWorld.motor` maintains arm targets via joint torque even with a zero root move command; a “root idle” condition is NOT a completely passive organism. For historical positions, active servo vs unpowered afterstate X differences included **0.2185 m** at `(17.1,11.1)` and **0.0872 m** at `(18,11.1)` (indirect motion included). Other tested cases were zero or signed differently.
- The enormous rigid-envelope rival cannot be treated as a clean anatomical comparator: several cases already have nonzero **first-step impulses** after re-rig (103.06, 132.18, 545.82), and direct contacts with root input OFF. Geometry surgery can introduce initial overlap. Exclude such comparisons from claims about action advantage. A truly fair morphology test would match relevant shape/reach and avoid injected overlap, not just total mass or bounding dimensions.
- In no-first-push worlds, object X may still move under later motor input **without measured second-body→target direct contact**; that is an indirect world interaction, not proof of new direct affordance.

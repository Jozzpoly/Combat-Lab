## 6. Major hidden harness confounds discovered

These are especially important because they make the first Ecology result scientifically inconclusive.

### Near-player spawn policy

Ordinary spawning first prefers a deterministic annulus around the current player.

This was introduced to fix an off-camera visual-rehearsal failure.

It improves visibility but also authors local pressure.

### Fallback spawn / traffic coupling

Each of the five fallback spawn zones contains one of the movement goals.

One contains goal 10, the strongest hidden traffic hub.

### Universal route hub

For ordinary spawns:

- initial goal assignment is balanced across the 12 goals;
- later ID/serial-based transition cycles are not.

Every `serial mod 12` route cycle contains goal 10:

- `(650,1580)`.

One twelfth of serial classes has a one-goal cycle there forever.

Under equal residue/cycle-visit weighting, goal 10 receives roughly **27.8%** of discrete cycle visits versus **8.3%** uniform.

Therefore the large lower-left jam has a strong harness-level feed mechanism and cannot be treated as endogenous crowd ecology.

### Serial confounds

Creation serial affects:

- spawn location hashes;
- initial goal;
- future route cycle.

Phenotype-wave comparisons are therefore not automatically matched stimulus comparisons.

### Steering bias

Static steering candidate order is asymmetrical:

- straight;
- positive angular offset;
- negative angular offset;
- etc.

Symmetric alternatives can inherit deterministic handedness.

### Sequential solver bias

Dynamic body pairs are resolved once in fixed array order.

Dense outcomes can therefore depend on actor creation/order and are not order-independent constraint solutions.

### Role confounds

Player vs residents differ in:

- controller;
- max speed;
- authored interaction pattern.

Do not attribute player-vs-crowd behavior to phenotype alone.

### Baseline confounds

Six heterogeneous baseline residents are each tied to unique:

- phenotype;
- position;
- ID;
- initial goal;
- route cycle.

They are possibility-rich visual seeds, not matched phenotype evidence.

## 7. Performance evidence — correct boundary

Late recording:

- 845 residents + player;
- ~357,435 pair checks per 120 Hz fixed step;
- roughly ~42.9M pair checks per simulated second;
- representative ~185k body contact-resolution hits/s;
- only about ~0.43% of all-pairs checks produce a contact resolution at that representative late state;
- first visible `SIM STRESS` appears only after repeated population escalation;
- late stress becomes sustained; simulation progresses at roughly ~0.81× wall-clock over the final stressed segment.

But this is a **whole-runtime result**, not a pure physics capacity number.

Performance is contaminated by:

- O(N²) dynamic pair loop;
- synchronous spawn-placement work;
- rendering every resident;
- full resident snapshot copies every render frame for browser evidence;
- diagnostics/UI/runtime overhead.

At 845 residents and ~60 fps, the machine-readable snapshot layer alone can create on the order of ~50k resident object copies/s.

Do not turn “~845 actors” into a capability promise.

If similar contact semantics survive later, spatial broad-phase pruning is a strong semantic-neutral optimization candidate — but do not optimize the obsolete solver before deciding whether its semantics deserve survival.

## 8. Important causal findings about phenotype controls

### Independent authoring is real Owner behavior

Owner repeatedly constructs staged phenotypes by altering one axis at a time while preserving others.

This strongly supports preserving raw independent dimensions.

### Body mass vs carried load are not yet separate mechanics

Current law immediately uses:

`totalMass = bodyMass + loadMass`.

Both then influence the same current acceleration/braking/contact-mass behavior.

Separate UI controls do **not** prove separate gameplay semantics yet.

### Locomotor force is an authority axis, not simply “speed”

Current force affects acceleration/braking/change-of-velocity authority while role max speed remains fixed.

Late Owner behavior keeps extremely high mass/load and later raises force dramatically.

This strengthens the principle that locomotor authority deserves to remain independent from mass and envelope.

### Live edits are apparatus interventions

Instant mass/radius/force edits preserve current velocity/world history.

They can create non-physical transients.

This is acceptable for Lab discovery.

It must remain distinguishable from endogenous world evolution and from controlled matched comparison.

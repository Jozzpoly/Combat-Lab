# Combat Lab — Frontier Reassessment 2026-09-26

**Status:** active top-down reassessment after Workbench+B0 closure  
**Authority:** strategic research synthesis subordinate to newest Owner evidence and `RESEARCH_STATE.md`  
**Implementation authority:** none yet; no new specimen is selected by this document alone

## 0. Why reassess now

Workbench+B0 closed the previous question well enough to move on.

What is now positively supported:

- the Workbench is usable for direct Owner research;
- body/world relations are materially perceptible;
- envelope, inertial burden/load and locomotor authority are worth keeping separable as underlying research dimensions;
- permissive unusual phenotypes are valuable;
- the project should not be rebuilt around its first positive melee/contact donor.

What remains open is no longer simply:

> can embodiment create visible differences?

The higher-value question is now:

> **do embodied differences remain decision-relevant when the world becomes richer and several bodies create active spatial pressure, or do they collapse into an interesting parameter sandbox / authored fit-not-fit puzzle?**

This reassessment starts from that uncertainty rather than from the old S1/R4 sequence.

## 1. Evidence that constrains the next frontier

### Positive evidence to preserve

From S0:

- body envelope changed route availability through material geometry rather than a hidden rule;
- alternate material bypass mattered;
- body mass changed body-body displacement;
- the Owner repeatedly returned to both geometry and contact relations;
- the body/world direction received a positive Owner signal.

From B0:

- envelope, body mass, carried load and locomotor force became independently manipulable;
- the Owner spontaneously constructed non-correlated phenotypes instead of only using intended anchors;
- the Workbench survived both extreme break-it exploration and finer tuning;
- final equations, units, collider shape and locomotor law remain unqualified.

From the wider failed combat campaign:

- mechanically real contact is not enough;
- authored lanes / policies can manufacture the intended answer;
- world geometry that does not change player choice becomes decoration;
- ordinary offense or free disengagement repeatedly bypassed interesting spatial relations;
- technical PASS must not be confused with useful play.

### Fresh Owner direction

The Owner now explicitly wants future exploration to include:

- optional, visible and reversible parameter linkage / correlated scaling for quickly authoring coherent phenotypes while preserving independent underlying axes;
- a larger world with more terrain and obstacles;
- more varied opponents / residents;
- more opportunities for pushing and being pushed;
- later donor recovery from Feniks, ReflexBrain, Companion and SPC for capabilities such as movement, pathfinding and spatial relations instead of needlessly reinventing them.

These are frontier-shaping inputs, not automatic implementation orders.

## 2. The current substrate is good enough — but exposes one new apparatus boundary

The current Workbench is adequate for another research campaign.

The current S0/B0 world representation is not.

Both experiments render by fitting the **entire world rectangle into the viewport**. Increasing the world dimensions would therefore mostly shrink the world visually rather than create a genuinely larger traversable space.

There is currently no shared camera / world-view abstraction in the core.

Implication:

> a genuinely larger spatial experiment needs an explicit observation/camera relation.

Do not promote a universal camera architecture prematurely.

For the first larger-world specimen, prefer an **experiment-owned camera/view transform** unless independent later experiments prove that a shared neutral helper is worth promoting.

## 3. Candidate frontier families

### A. Active embodied spatial ecology

Question:

> When several differently embodied bodies have independent movement intent inside varied terrain, do envelope, inertia/load and locomotor authority change what the Owner chooses to do?

Potential player-visible relations:

- hold vs yield;
- push vs be displaced;
- angle / bypass;
- pass against opposing flow;
- exploit or avoid clearance constraints;
- occupy or surrender local space;
- use open space differently from clutter / choke / obstacle fields.

Why it is high-value now:

- directly pressures the positive S0/B0 finding;
- upgrades the evidence target from **human-discriminable** toward **decision-relevant**;
- tests body/world value across multiple geometry families instead of one authored choke;
- introduces multiple actors without yet requiring a combat engine;
- naturally creates the conditions in which optional phenotype linkage may become useful as an authoring convenience.

Primary risks:

- becoming a crowd simulator detached from combat possibility;
- replacing the old corridor puzzle with scripted traffic puzzles;
- hiding the answer inside NPC policy;
- letting primitive collision artifacts dominate the result;
- expanding actor AI before the body/world question earns it.

### B. Posture / stance-dependent occupied space

Question:

> Can one actor materially alter its world/contact relation through stance without changing class identity?

High relevance to Feniks and strong Owner interest.

Why not lead immediately:

- current circle body and qualitative contact solver make stance semantics easy to fake with hidden modifiers;
- the result would remain relatively local unless pressured by richer terrain and multiple bodies;
- active spatial ecology can expose where stance would actually solve a real problem instead of inventing one for it.

Status:

> **strong follow-up candidate, not current lead.**

### C. Equipment as body extension

Question:

> Does carried/worn geometry change how an actor inhabits space before it changes damage numbers?

High value for shields, armour, long tools and burden.

Why not lead immediately:

- B0 has only separated load mass, not spatial equipment;
- a richer body/world pressure field gives equipment something meaningful to interact with;
- otherwise equipment risks becoming another isolated mechanism demo.

Status:

> **strong follow-up / cross-modal bridge.**

### D. Ranged / bow or magic

These remain important orthogonal fronts.

Why not lead immediately:

- the actor/world substrate has only just produced its first positive human evidence;
- ranged and magic would immediately introduce new action/consequence semantics while the decision relevance of embodiment is still unresolved;
- previous ranged evidence showed that projectile mechanics can collapse into delete/kite/suppress loops when the surrounding exchange is weak.

Status:

> **valuable later divergent combat fronts; premature as the immediate frontier.**

### E. Optional correlated scaling

This is not a research frontier by itself.

It is a Workbench authoring capability:

> preserve independent authored dimensions, then optionally let a visible relationship profile drive selected dimensions together.

Examples might later include:

- size -> mass;
- size -> locomotor force;
- load -> acceleration authority;
- arbitrary custom relationships.

Do not implement a generic dependency graph now.

First let a multi-phenotype experiment create real authoring friction; then implement the smallest linkage mechanism that removes that friction without hiding causality.

### F. Cross-project donor recovery

This is also not a research frontier by itself.

It is an execution strategy to use once the next question creates a concrete dependency.

Trigger examples:

- active residents need nontrivial pathfinding;
- a larger world needs already-proven navigation/spatial bookkeeping;
- movement/controller behavior would otherwise be reinvented;
- multi-actor neighborhood queries become material.

At that point, inspect Feniks / ReflexBrain / Companion / SPC for the **specific needed capability**, recover provenance/evidence, transplant minimally and re-qualify inside Combat Lab.

Do not perform a broad donor architecture import before the question is selected.

## 4. Provisional lead

The current highest-information frontier is:

> **ACTIVE EMBODIED SPATIAL ECOLOGY / MULTI-BODY PRESSURE**

This is a provisional research lead, not yet an implementation command.

The reason is evidence convergence:

1. S0 showed body/world relations are perceptible;
2. B0 showed their major underlying axes can be manipulated independently;
3. the Owner now asks for larger spaces, more terrain and varied opponents/residents;
4. historical whole-combat failures repeatedly showed that material relations only matter if they change player choice under pressure;
5. active multi-body pressure is the smallest next step that attacks this exact uncertainty without prematurely restoring weapons/damage/AI architecture.

## 5. What the first specimen should ask — before designing features

Working question:

> **When several bodies with materially different envelope / inertia / locomotor authority pursue simple independent movement intents inside a varied traversable world, do those differences cause the Owner to naturally choose different spatial behavior?**

Potential 30-second discriminator:

Without role labels, telemetry instructions or a scripted “correct” route, the Owner begins doing materially different things such as:

- yielding to one body but pushing another;
- taking an angle or bypass because direct passage is costly;
- holding local space with one phenotype but avoiding the same relation with another;
- exploiting open terrain differently from clutter;
- changing approach because another body's envelope / inertia / movement authority actually matters.

The desired observation is not:

> “these NPCs have different stats.”

It is:

> **“I naturally behave differently around these bodies and this terrain.”**

## 6. Hard falsifiers

Stop or refound if:

- the result is merely B0 with a larger background;
- body differences still matter only at hand-authored gaps;
- every resident follows a route that encodes the intended answer;
- the same optimal movement works for all phenotypes;
- labels/presets are needed to explain which body should push/yield;
- agent/pathfinding sophistication becomes the research object;
- the Owner spends time debugging collisions/camera instead of probing relations;
- the experiment needs weapons/damage to make spatial relations matter;
- larger space reduces readability instead of creating new possibilities;
- the current qualitative contact solver produces artifacts large enough to dominate the decision.

## 7. Minimal apparatus implications

Likely required for a first bounded specimen:

- an experiment-owned camera / larger traversable world view;
- several geometry families in one space rather than one discriminating choke;
- a transparent active-body population with a small deterministic baseline and permissive live population escalation;
- several materially distinct phenotypes using the existing B0 dimensions;
- deterministic reset;
- fast reconfiguration / restart;
- enough observation to diagnose mechanics without turning the Owner UI into a dashboard.

Likely **not** required yet:

- damage / HP;
- weapons;
- combat AI;
- classes;
- full pathfinding stack before simple intent proves insufficient;
- generic actor editor;
- generic parameter dependency graph;
- production collision/physics;
- final NPC behavior;
- final Feniks camera.

## 8. First adversarial comparison — provisional lead survives

The provisional lead was challenged against three materially different alternatives.

| Frontier | What it would answer now | Main problem now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active embodied spatial ecology | whether B0/S0 embodiment changes Owner decisions under live world/body pressure | can collapse into a movement/crowd sandbox if pressure is weak or scripted |
| Stance / posture | whether one actor can materially alter occupied-space/contact relation | likely adds another embodiment variable before we know where decision pressure actually needs it |
| Equipment as body extension | whether shields/armour/tools materially alter spatial occupancy before damage | depends on having richer world/body relations for equipment to interact with; otherwise risks mechanism-demo isolation |
| Bow / magic | whether a very different combat modality escapes melee history | introduces action/consequence semantics before the current positive body/world finding has proved decision relevance |

Result:

> **Active embodied spatial ecology remains the strongest immediate research lead.**

This is not because it is the most important final Feniks system.

It survives because it asks the narrowest high-value question created by the evidence we already have:

> **does embodiment change behavior once other bodies and terrain push back?**

### Why stance does not win yet

Stance has strong Owner relevance and may become a major Feniks relation.

But if we implement brace/squeeze/defensive posture now, we can easily produce a satisfying local mechanism without learning whether the existing envelope/inertia/locomotor distinctions matter in a broader living situation.

A richer pressure field can instead reveal concrete failure cases such as:

- “I need to narrow my profile here”;
- “I want to brace because this flow displaces me”;
- “I want to yield contact without losing the route.”

Those are better reasons to open stance than abstract completeness.

### Why equipment does not win yet

Equipment should eventually extend body truth.

But shields, armour, long tools and carried objects become more informative when:

- clearance already matters in several places;
- other bodies contest space;
- burden and envelope have visible consequences;
- the player has real reasons to protect / screen / pass / hold.

The pressure field can therefore create the situation in which equipment becomes a grounded next question.

### Why bow / magic do not win yet

They remain essential anti-tunnel candidates.

However, opening them immediately would confound:

- embodied movement;
- active body pressure;
- aiming / preparation;
- line of sight;
- projectile or field semantics;
- consequence;
- disengagement.

That is a much larger hypothesis bundle.

The historical ranged campaign already showed how easy it is for a real projectile mechanism to collapse into delete / kite / suppress behavior.

The body/world finding should first prove it can shape choice without needing damage to become significant.

## 9. Candidate apparatus contract — still pre-implementation

If the provisional lead survives the next design pass, the first specimen should be bounded by the following apparatus principles.

### World

Not “make a huge map.”

Instead:

- one continuous traversable space large enough that the whole world cannot simply be scaled into the viewport as S0/B0 do;
- several materially different spatial regions in the same world;
- at least:
  - open space;
  - clutter / obstacle field;
  - constrained passage with more than one viable relation / bypass;
- no single authored gate that explains the entire experiment.

A simple player-following camera may be owned by the experiment initially.

### Population

Use a **small, readable deterministic baseline**, but do **not** make that baseline a population cap.

The Owner must be able to increase pressure directly during play. Minimum intended controls:

- Spawn +1;
- Spawn +5;
- Spawn +10;
- Clear extras;
- Reset World back to the deterministic baseline.

Repeated spawning into crowd / horde / break regimes is valid discovery, not misuse.

The apparatus should support:

- sparse pairwise encounters;
- concurrency;
- crossing flow;
- local congestion;
- sustained crowd pressure;
- deliberate horde / stress states;
- repeated pushing/yielding opportunities;
- variation in envelope / inertia / locomotor authority.

Do not add combat intelligence merely to make the population feel active.

If performance, collision quality or readability breaks first, classify that break honestly instead of hiding it behind a low population limit.

### Resident intent

Residents need **independent movement intent**, but that intent should be transparent and intentionally weak.

Candidate principle:

> deterministic destination-seeking / local movement goals that create pressure incidentally rather than policies that know the research answer.

Do not encode:

- “heavy actor should hold here”;
- “small actor should take this gap”;
- player-countering tactics;
- hidden role/class policy.

If nontrivial pathfinding becomes necessary to keep this apparatus from getting stuck, that is the trigger for targeted donor recovery before inventing another navigation stack.

### Phenotypes

Start from the B0 dimensions:

- envelope;
- body mass / carried load where useful;
- locomotor force / authority.

Use several deliberately different configurations plus at least one awkward/non-correlated phenotype.

Do not label them “tank”, “rogue”, “heavy”, etc.

Optional correlated scaling remains deferred unless repeated multi-actor authoring creates real friction.

### Contact

The current collision law is only qualitative evidence.

Before Owner exposure, red-team:

- dense pair / small-cluster contacts;
- wall + body contact;
- high mass ratios;
- high locomotor authority;
- no explosive/jitter-dominated states.

If the qualitative solver dominates play, fix or bound the apparatus.

Do not turn that repair into a production physics campaign.

### Human gate

The first human question should remain simple:

> **Do I naturally start treating different bodies / routes / spaces differently, without being told what role each one has?**

If not, stop before adding stance, equipment, weapons or AI sophistication.

## 10. Owner correction — population pressure is permissive

The phrase “small population, not a crowd benchmark” was too restrictive and contradicted the Owner's established research contract.

Correct interpretation:

> **small is the default for legibility; it is not the permission boundary.**

The Owner explicitly wants direct controls such as `Spawn +1 / +5 / +10` and the ability to keep escalating until the experiment breaks.

This changes the frontier positively:

- population count/density becomes a research variable;
- sparse → multi-body → dense → horde regimes may expose qualitatively different world/body relations;
- technical breakdown thresholds are evidence when distinguished from actual actor/world behavior;
- any true population safety rail must be broad, explicit and justified only by runtime/browser stability.

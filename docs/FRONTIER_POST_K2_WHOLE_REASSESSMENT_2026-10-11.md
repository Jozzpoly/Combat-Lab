## Critical new alternative — top-down support does NOT have to demand 3D
Official Box2D 2.4 explicitly defines a **Friction Joint for top-down friction** providing 2D translational and angular resistance, with finite `maxForce` and `maxTorque`: https://box2d.org/doc_version_2_4/classb2_friction_joint.html . The official Box2D dynamics documentation also describes it as a top-down friction joint: https://box2d.org/doc_version_2_4/md__e_1_2github_2box2d__24_2docs_2dynamics.html .

This is NOT evidence that the current Rapier2D JS version has an equivalent API or that importing Box2D is optimal. It provides a credible *research rival* to either full 3D ground contact or unconstrained/free 2D top-down drive: finite explicit virtual-ground reaction, separately accounting for force and torque (perhaps physical support permission/terrain), **not** infinite positional pinning or pretending the root's own muscles generate external force. A minimal owned impulse-law implementation would also be a rival, but must openly track its ground-reaction impulse and obey finite budgets. It must not silently become an ungrounded braking cheat. Real foot slip, support/normal and grounded control may still require higher fidelity if gameplay pressure justifies them.

## Competing hypotheses for the actual whole-level bottleneck
- **H-WORLD (material relational opportunities):** existing scenes have too little mutable meaningful topology. A varied but coherent place with live gates, movable masses, independent channels and persistent material afterstate could let different bodies create *different subsequent actions*. Falsify with a cheap root/body, not authored puzzle locks.
- **H-SUPPORT (force source & stance):** real limits to bracing/yielding are masked by absent ground reaction. Candidate comparison: cheap explicit finite planar friction/body support, genuine 3D grounded source, ungrounded null. Falsify by showing more support fidelity brings only larger counters/stability and no practical body-world difference.
- **H-EFFECTOR (independent action):** K2 single shield could not produce a unique category; perhaps simultaneous support+manipulation needs distinct reach/leverage/force source. Must beat a simple rigid mass/envelope/action control, not a weak stationary decoy.
- **H-INTERFACE:** sparse/poorly readable crude material yard may hide possibilities behind UI, not just physics. But polish cannot make a non-existent material verb real. First real mouse/keyboard manipulation evidence is still missing on K2; raw frame/DOM PASS is not Owner usability.

**Do not sequentially build four test racks.** An independently defensible next construction candidate is ONE **freely editable material pressure workshop**: multiple visible paths and loads, a physically yielding/pivoting gate or obstruction whose position matters to other actors, at least two bodies with concretely unlike interventions (move/sustain/bypass/deflect), and a cheap competitor able to solve the same situation if physics permits. Per-body initiative, if added, should respond to local material facts, not destination smells or choreographed roles. User should be able to throw loads, reposition bodies, break setup and discover a second consequence without following a puzzle route. Support should start with the cheapest *honest* bounded candidate, not assumed 3D.

**No green acceptance target like '3 verbs'**. The thing worth earning is meaningful, visibly different body↔matter continuations and genuine replayable experimentation, not another array of fixture PASSes.

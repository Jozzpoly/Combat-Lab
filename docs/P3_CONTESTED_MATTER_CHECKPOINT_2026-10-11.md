# P3 · One shared load, two independently driven bodies (2026-10-11)

**Classification:** ISOLATED exploratory P3 donor, **physical solver-level multi-body material relation demonstrated**, NOT owner-qualified gameplay, morphology superiority, NPC autonomy, or selected host. Branch `experiment/p3-contested-material-agency` grew from P2 only as research dependency, not as a promotion of P0/P2. Historical R0/R1/L0 Owner FAIL remains binding. Public `rehearsal/current` untouched.

## Why this candidate
P2 proved a finite, real solver-contact–gated grip could drag separately simulated cargo; equal-speed red-team reversed the naïve “large naturally better than small” inference. P3 asks whether the *same independent* cargo and its two material holders can support opposed simultaneous forces, competition and physical relinquishment, rather than just one operator pulling one inert box. This is a different physical relationship but NOT, by itself, proof of useful living world or autonomous conflict.

## Implemented
- Two physical P0 root bodies approach a 45kg independent Rapier2D rectangle **from opposite sides**, with finite explicitly labeled **operator-supplied** root-drive impulses. The source protocol does not make actors navigate on their own.
- Link acquisition is only allowed after a solver `contactPair` manifold between each real body and the cargo. `TransportYard.grip` in this branch permits two *distinct* physical actors to hold the same dynamic load. One actor may not stack multiple grips; a bounded two-link maximum is **a research scope limit**, not a final game restriction. Real contact must precede BOTH grips.
- Each link retains its own physical anchor relative to actor and cargo, applies a separately accounted equal/opposite capped spring–damper impulse each solver tick, can break under excessive stretch, and can be removed by its holder. The cargo is **never kinematically reposed**, and continues interacting with real independent colliders. No inventory object, teleport, invisible win state, fixed body position, or disabled actor–matter collision.
- Interactive isolated P3 browser: **“Dwa ciała · jeden ciężar”** resets to a simple physical A/B/cargo situation; **WASD** controls actor A, **IJKL** actor B, **G/H** individually attempt contact-authenticated grip/release, adjustable impulse strength, normal sandbox editing/physics. The browser labels A/B and renders actual dynamic bodies and visible (schematic center-to-center) grip links. There is **no automatic opponent decision** or fake “NPC”.

## Counterfactuals, bounded evidence
- **24 actual emitted Chromium/Rapier2D WASM physical worlds**, paired across virtual support `finite`/`none`, source left-actor morphology small/bulky, and policies `off`, `left`, `right`, `dual`, `zero`, `release`. All use actual bilateral source contact before stage change, and zero-link null never transfers grip impulse. No claims of energy-equivalent matched grip rivals.
- Example both small, finite support, 45kg: independently applying left-only grip leads cargo x10.217; right-only x10.583; BOTH opposite real grips keep it approximately x10.4005; no grip remains x10.400. Actual grip impulses are positive when links exert force and zero for off/zero models. In `none` terrain, opposed balanced links also largely retain central mass position, while left- or right-only transport moves far to either side. **The zero cargo motion of a duel is NOT itself proof of unique emergent gameplay** — cheap equal forces can cancel.
- Native Chromium CDP test at [initial source CI 38103409619](https://github.com/Jozzpoly/Combat-Lab/actions/runs/38103409619) (before latest A/B tightening): true independent user-facing **G and H** keyboard acquisition yielded two links without moving cargo, actual held **A and L** resisted one another with load x10.3993 and positive 96.1865 N·s total grip impulse, then native **H** released the right link, kept **A** and source cargo traveled to x9.9219, with accumulated impulse333.3862N·s. Two **raw real Chrome screenshots** before and after release plus actual full JSON held in short-retention Actions artifact. This is real keyboard/mouse input, NOT synthetic DOM event firing. Images are still simple circles and rectangle, not hands/characters or gameplay feel.
- **Critical causal correction made during the run:** initial diagnostic `release` scenario both disconnected B **and stopped commanding its movement**, so any afterstate could not be attributed solely to link release. Corrected protocol now keeps the same actor movement inputs and same pre-release physical states in linked-vs-released A/B, changing **only B's physical link**. The latest exact-source CI and structured `releaseContrasts` must be verified before claiming an effect size from that improved comparison. The separate native experiment already held A motion and lifted B's control after link release; this test too does NOT isolate link release only, so it is interaction evidence, not fully controlled causality.
- P3 uses P2 finite *virtual* terrain impulse for top-down ground-reaction proxy, not physical leg-ground mechanics. Anatomy A/B changes in a few controls do not show unique body actions. Body root velocities and motors remain input-policy dependent.
- No visual or mechanical Owner first look/PASS yet. Real Chrome source screenshots are trustworthy for geometry and movement but do not tell us subjective feel, 3D fidelity, real time FPS, body intent, or crowded-system quality.

## Source-relative limits
1. Two identical opposing externally commanded drives are a clean dynamical fixture, not genuinely reacting actors.
2. Grip source uses a **shared center-midpoint anchor approximation**, not fingers/contact-surface grasp with local friction cones. The rendered visual link is schematic. Whether this looks/feels convincing is OPEN.
3. The two-link cap exists only for controlled source scope. It is not a legitimate content-design law.
4. Contact manifold `numSolverContacts` proves solver found a contact, but does not prove anatomically plausible holding force. Legitimate sustained grip vs rope-like spring still open.
5. Balanced cargo remaining central is also observed with **off/zero controls**; the value is active, persistent, physically contestable relationship and release semantics, not uniquely novel location. Judge the causal difference with the corrected link-only release pair and cheap pushing comparator.
6. Do not turn this into a predetermined authored goal/quest to manufacture “interesting” behavior or another loop of 5-minute tuning.

## Decision / continuation
P3 is a **meaningful narrow force/matter interaction donor**, potentially worth further exploratory pressure in a more legible freely editable situation, but still not enough for a *living world* or Owner gameplay qualification. Next serious question is whether organically distinct body strategies, group friction/pressure, persistence, and simple local reactions produce real unexpected practical consequences under Owner intervention.

## Mission / primary falsifier

P2 established true solver-contact, finite-impulse transport; P3 established two independent actors exerting opposing grips on *one actual independently simulated body*, with real G/H keyboard, but all actor intent was supplied by the source operator. P4 established a cheap load-velocity follow reflex (conditional sensor did not beat unconditional cheap follow) and falsified angular conservation claims of the original vector damping. A physically more accountable central/axial compliant tether retained the basic P2/P3 hauling consequences in **96 real solver-world A/B regressions**.

P5 investigates a different hypothesis: can an editable *whole* with 6–10 different physical bodies, several independent colliding loads, persistent world obstruction, and minimal local, contact-driven sensorimotor state exhibit non-scripted downstream **material consequences**? The plan explicitly attempts to falsify this with the same world under simple cheap locomotion, touch-only, grip-only and grip-plus-follow policies. P5 is a **local interaction mechanism laboratory**, NOT a claim of full autonomous living NPC or a new Combat Lab product baseline.

## Material substrate and actual source local action

`src/p5-commons.js` creates six (optional ten) independent real Rapier2D rigid bodies (mix small 27kg, bulky 160kg), three independent physical masses (45/70/24kg) plus two fixed obstacles inside the inherited P0 material courtyard with a genuine gate. Persistent dynamic bodies have ordinary collision. No challenge completion trigger, target coordinates, global path map, planted NPC win state or scripted interaction timeline. The body *initial positions and forward directions* are deliberately authored to generate mechanical pressure; this remains an experimenter prior, and none of these movers independently has a goal.

Each body has a declared local role:
- PRESS: continue its finite virtual traction drive in its own heading.
- YIELD: actual collision manifold causes a bounded heading revision based on *the physically contacting partner*, never on an omniscient enemy waypoint.
- BRACE: at actual forceful contact, temporary finite resistance of body momentum through explicitly accounted **virtual ground** stance, no positional lock.
- CARRIER: at a *real Rapier contactPair solver manifold* with an independent cargo, attempt limited compliant **central/axial grip**, then continue finite forward drive or use the cheapest P4 cargo-follow response, depending on policy. No instant grab from visual proximity, teleporting load, inventory, scripted rendezvous, actual fingers, hand/weld or mechanical advantage of anatomy.

All decisions take place in `localStep`, driven by actual prior-step physical contacts and organism local state, not a stage clock or scripted opposition. Opposing bodies and previously moved load positions can change encounters. Four strategy controls: `constant` (cheap identical initial local drives), `touch` (actual contacts and local give/brace but no automatic grips), `attach` (same local touch plus real grips, no cheap follow), `material` (touch+grip+bounded follow). These are **not work/energy matched**: different policies may exert different total root proxy efforts. The world uses P4 central axial-link alternative locally, not the original leaking transverse P2/P3 force.

## Critical limits

- No actual self-chosen goals or full creature cognition. Initial heading seeded and maintained by simple steering; touch/brace/grip rules are narrow author-specified local reflexes. No real eyes/ears, semantic world perception, memory or planning.
- Virtual traction is a source-accounted top-down impulse, NOT gait, footholds, real motor biomechanics, energy/metabolism, or properly grounded friction. Initial geometry and scalar speeds/masses are part of behavior; no proven special morphology advantage. Touch-count counters count **per body-observed active solver contact per tick**, not unique encounters.
- Different variant total drive work/effort is **not controlled**; categorical link acquisition is clean (touch never links, grip policies only after solver contact), but *all subsequent position contrasts* are coaffected by altered steering/traction and collision history. Do not claim a superior morphology, intelligence or universal behavior.
- Other substrate debt: midpoint physically local anchor is a cheap rope-like approximation, axial force has no independent torsion, at most two grip links per cargo as current experiment scope. Full torque/joint semantics and actual hand-like mechanism remain unresolved. No pathing or meaningful body sensory legibility yet.
- No real FPS/stress assessment or independent Owner first look. Even a huge matrix of green CI tests cannot overrule historical Owner FAIL.
- Editable barriers are true fixed colliders and movable by explicit Owner action while paused; permanent outer bounds/hinge mount remain protected, not a fully general authored world editing system. Dynamic actor/cargo move while paused via inherited P0 tools. No claim all world material is editable.

## Next judgement

P5 has materially stronger whole-world causality than P0/P2/P3 isolated setups, without staged actor input every tick. But the system remains mostly rudimentary small local automata and broad rigid circles, visually still primitive. Next research should test user-driven natural manipulation and emergent pressure in genuinely different topology/density cases, and body shape versus cheap control comparison, **only where it changes the actual Owner experience**. Hold or reject P5 as donor after lived browser trial; do not merge its implementation into main or public just because exact-source CI is green. Keep the dual blocked/open collision topology and source traces as reproducible material falsifiers. Owner should never need to manually reconstruct this run when switching chats.

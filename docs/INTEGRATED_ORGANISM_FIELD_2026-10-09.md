## Why this exists

The previous Rapier Yard did real work on finite motor control, contact evidence, editable matter and the human workbench. However its basic bodies and actor-private shuttle can remain phenomenologically near the old crossing-ball scene, despite numerous valid mechanical checks. The new specimen is **not an iteration of that shuttle**. It is a fresh alternative from neutral main that deliberately changes physical morphology, contact distribution, turning and world interaction together.

## Working question and contrasts

Does free intervention in one 2D material world expose differences that cannot be explained merely by radius, speed, or authored path?

Three intentionally contrasting **physical prototypes**, not species:
- compact dart: low mass, narrow contact hull, high steering/drive authority;
- articulated crawler: front and rear independent dynamic masses connected by a real revolute joint, internal reciprocal bend actuation, long footprint, separate collision response;
- broad pusher: broad rigid hull, higher mass, poorer turning, offset front contact.

Shared conditions: persistent displaced crates, uneven passages plus open space, explicit low-traction region, other physical organisms, Owner-added walls/matter and off-centre impulse intervention. Local automatic actors see only previous-step contact and realized progress; they cannot see the World geometry, map, object IDs or intended routes. Selected actor can be controlled directly.

## The critical modeling boundary

Top-down 2D has no implicit supporting ground plane. Locomotion uses an **explicit finite external traction impulse budget** with a spatial traction coefficient. This is a simplifying motor-ground coupling, not a claim of native Rapier foot support, gait, torque-to-ground conversion, crawling biomechanics or soft-body physics. The articulated body really bends/contact-collides; its reciprocal internal bend impulse alone cannot drive net free-space travel. The substrate impulse supplies locomotion.

Compound colliders and joints are world-side physical facts. Contact for behavior requires solver-active manifolds. Actor policy does not receive world IDs/locations. Intervention logs/contacts remain researcher-only.

3. Unusual geometry/mass/actor counts and overlapping material must be stress tested without covert teleportation or hidden despawn. Honest throughput metrics required before claims of scale.
4. A world alteration must create genuinely different continuations for at least two body morphologies in a shared unscripted situation.
5. Human-facing usability must be substantial enough to explore; an impressive CI is not a reason to request Owner judgment on an empty or broken world.

### Serious limitations / next research pressure

The current body/motor law is an authored finite XY **ground-reaction approximation**, not leg/foot locomotion, support mechanics or realistic multi-limbed gait. The crawler's real revolute segment is dynamically material but remains propulsion-assisted by this approximation. Only three *families* of hull/contact geometry exist, albeit with independent mass, length, width and actuator authoring. The local behaviors are small pressure/recovery heuristics, not a credible organism ecology.

The initial browser surface has headless boot and measurement evidence, **not** Owner visual/readability/feel evidence. It has no scientific proof of whole-life richness, long-time contact robustness, material object ecology or scale. The next honest test is whether genuinely different, independently varied morphology/authority combinations make the person running a freely disturbed world discover several unforeseen actions and constraints — versus a collection of pleasant but still shallow moving props. If the latter, redesign rather than accrete more tests or dashboards.

## Actual source and controls

`src/p7-crowd.js` builds Rapier2D zero-gravity world (finite/none controlled virtual terrain), two real fixed barrier colliders making a 2.85m/3.45m/4.65m aperture and 2 independent movable cargo. A **DYNAMIC**, not fixed, defender is a circle of radius1.15m (large) or .44m (small) with **actual collider mass set to 90kg** in both geometries. 3/6/8 independent small radius.44m pusher bodies of native27kg have equal 470N bounded motor command and 1.85m/s target, initially heading only +X. Distinct starting rows have authored positions but no path graph, global destination or scripted interventions. This is **finite virtual root traction, not feet/real anatomy**.

`passive`: guard receives no secret root drive or world-coordinate locking; only real physics and finite terrain support. `brace`: guard changes stance ONLY after **real Rapier solver contact manifold** from a physically touching pusher with source impulse >.12 N.s. Under finite ground this increases actual bounded ground reaction; it does not make the body immovable. Under no-ground control it is merely a label without magic counter-force. Crowds have cheap `drive` plus a one-time `yield` after ACTUAL guard impact, steering diagonally as a low-cognition comparator. The latter does not implement anticipatory pathing, body negotiation or planning.

Owner can enter P7 through an actual browser button, choose 3/6/8 and guard radius, gap and reaction mode, change rules without resetting world, apply a real off-center impulse to the defender, pause, move **genuine fixed barriers with Ctrl+mouse or the audited widen action**, resume the same bodies. New P7 focus hides earlier experiment toolbar while preserving prior P0–P6 source UI when not in P7; this is strictly presentational. Click a physically rendered defender, use **WASD** for root input and **B** to change contact stance; direct selection overrides automatic guard stance source so Owner input is not silently undone. Debug contact lines represent actual **solver-contact participants and measured summed impulse**, but lines join body centers and are NOT contact point geometry. The renderer remains colored circles and rectangles; human feel remains UNKNOWN.

## True physical worlds and scoped findings

`src/p7-probe.js`: 64 factorial 2-width ×2-density ×2-support ×2-size ×2-guard-mode ×2-crowd-mode real solver worlds, plus 12 paired operator interventions (same complete pre-action physical state) in central formation, plus 16 single-lateral-start physical worlds and 6 additional paired operator intervention worlds in lateral formation: **98 physical source worlds**. Uses real dynamic solver state, contact impulses and individual cargo afterstate, not authored outcomes.

**Negative result important:** with 2.85m aperture and six centrally driven actors under finite source terrain, passive guard results in six of six pushers **ending beyond choke threshold and inside aperture interval**; source guard x19.7549. Contact-triggered BRACE yields only four of six, but the 90kg defender STILL moves strongly, ending x22.0597, with markedly greater cumulative contact and pusher motor effort. This falsifies the simplistic claim that `brace` literally holds a doorway. Worse cheap `yield` under this pressure gives 0/6 in both guard controls, while spending more root motor effort. In these initial central lanes changing width2.85→4.65 makes no difference in passive/no-ground because the group never stressed side clearance: avoid calling that a test of meaningful geometry. Source contact counters count actor-observed contact in individual simulation ticks, not unique meetings or unique organisms.

**Lateral geometry falsifier:** one of the otherwise identical six source pushers begins y13.68, driving +X at same limited motor. Under finite ground with SMALL dynamic 90kg guard and PASSIVE mode, the 2.85m throat physically blocks it at x14.2401 (real collider), even as five other bodies end past source readout. With only a wider 4.65m authored throat, same lateral body ends x23.8142 and all six end beyond the mouth. Under a BIG 90kg guard and same wide gap the lateral body can also pass but interactions change its afterstate; the effect is physically conditional, not globally scripted. **Important limit**: actor did not discover that route; the initial y-line was deliberately authored to stress side clearance.

**Operator counterfactual:** identical complete source pre-intervention states at tick210 in every matched no-op/physical wall move/85N.s real off-center defender impulse group, preserving actual positions, velocities, rotations, rules/effort. With narrow center-lane BIG brace, real widening can change observed readout from4→5; with narrow lateral BIG brace, from3→4, while dynamically changing guard/cargo consequences. Many controls are neutral (e.g., already-wide and passive), an important negative. These are **nonlinear afterstates** across different terrain root effort histories, not proof that a stance controller is uniformly optimal.

**Crucial self-audit of incorrect donor:** original P7 `openPassage()` mistakenly MOVED THE UPPER wall DOWN by1.30m, which **narrowed** the physical throat despite a UI label saying `open`. Initial apparent adverse results were INVALID as evidence of widening. Fixed explicitly to move the LOWER wall down and derive gap width from LIVE collider positions. Browser and source assert actual measured Rapier aperture grows ~1.30m. The original negative stands as a methodological error, never claim it was a discovery about NPC defense. Another CI failure was strict f32 1e-7m tolerance despite correct WASM geometry; source now asserts <=0.1mm with actual dimensions in failure message.

**Native Chromium source:** one genuine headless Chrome CDP session (not synthetic DOM PointerEvent) clicks P7 preset, picks the real movable defender by physical on-canvas coordinates, switches posture and provides actual D key motor input, resets by P7 browser button, then runs actual 7-actor/2-load source, physically shocks defender, pauses, widens real collider, resumes same world. ## Interpretation / next real opportunity

P7's new value is a **material pressure field observable in a genuinely shared space with operator interference**, not more examples of hard circle geometry or single-cargo towing. P6 local angle/jam recovery is not automatically wired into P7 actors; P7 does NOT have global navigation, local wall anticipation, body-specific deformability, adaptive crowd shoving, multi-limb gait or meaningful NPC goals. A big 90kg circle and small 90kg circle diverge on physical geometry, but that is a CHEAP SOLVER primitive; expensive articulation/softbody have NOT earned adoption.

Potential next pressure should prioritize **quality of interacting with crowd and forgiving physical contacts** through a genuinely usable live browser scene and Owner intervention, not tuning 100 more numerical cases. Test soft/stance-dependent contact only if it solves an observed obstruction/rudeness issue; compare cheap purely rigid rivals, and don't undo material causality with invisible bypass. Human feel, legibility, identity, player agency and accessibility can veto mechanical novelty. Source-only tests are not an artistic endorsement.

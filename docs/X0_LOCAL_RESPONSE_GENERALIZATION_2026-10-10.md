# X0 — across-scene local-response falsification (2026-10-10)

**Status:** isolated experimental evidence, not an Owner-level X0 PASS, not autonomous actor behavior, not authority to merge or deploy. Earlier Owner FAILs are unchanged. The source is `src/x0-local-response-falsifier.js` on draft PR #4.

## The bounded question

Does a *physically sourced, local tactile limb reflex* actually change the shared world after an unscripted material contact — and is that enough to change another organism's own reaction or available action? Earlier 95-step/contact counts and impressive material travel could not answer this. We therefore tested nine **predeclared** repositions of actors 0 and 1 around the same real hinged material source, **360 solver ticks** each. The world-powered hinge is the explicitly authored energy source; nobody is directly possessed and no point impulses are injected. This is a deliberately narrow test of *causal reaction*, not autonomous initiative or a magic solved scenario.

## Contrasts and results — emitted Chromium / Rapier

**A. All tactile arm reflexes ON vs OFF.** Finite body geometry, initial state, all local bracing, world motor, solver and material remain unchanged. A response occurs in **9/9** on cases; in **8/9** the final material location differs by over 0.1 m, and in **7/9** at least one actor's root location differs by over 0.1 m. Maximum measured end-of-run material displacement contrast: **3.788 m**. For tested checkpoints, trajectories coincide **before** the different local actuator command and diverge after it. This is genuine limited body-local control affecting physical downstream material, not evidence of a different *kind of action*.

**B. Ablate ONLY actor 0's reflex.** Other organisms retain their real own tactile response in both matched worlds. Actor 1's *first response timing* differs in **2/9** cases: 136 vs 137 ticks and 144 vs 150 ticks. The **number** of actor-1 reflexes remains **the same** (one) in these comparisons. Another tested condition showed identical actor-1 response at tick 125, *before* actor 0's response at tick 155. Therefore do **not** attribute that earlier second response to the first organism.

**C. Actual second-body finite hold eligibility.** After 360 ticks, without moving anything into a convenient position, select the same second organism and ask which actual material colliders could receive a near-touch contact hold from its real collider. In **all 9 matched cases**, eligibility was empty in both worlds. **0/9** cases provided a new or lost hold action. This tests one specific, geometrically tolerant contact-hold verb, **not** every possible physical action (such as pushing, passing, blocking or hitting).

At [emitted browser CI for code before final report](https://github.com/Jozzpoly/Combat-Lab/actions/runs/38013609883) the full comparison completed successfully. Source-ref and SHA must be re-checked if the branch advances.

## What survived / what failed

- **SURVIVED (bounded):** a genuine tactile reflex can produce materially different shared-world afterstates over several nearby configurations. This is more robust than a single scripted afterstate.
- **WEAK/CONDITIONAL:** it sometimes changes *when* another actor reacts, but there is no new kind of later actor behavior. The world motor supplies the initial energy and the chosen encounters are authored initial conditions.
- **NOT DEMONSTRATED:** any new physical action available to that second actor in these nine situations. The earlier two-stage "second action" candidate was independently falsified by an idle-actor control and remains negative.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** any Owner-interest or rich first-run possibilities. Nine authored parameter variations are not an ecological/generalization proof, an emergent goal, or human feedback.

## Strategic consequence — stop polishing F2

Do **not** add another refined local contact threshold, reflex counter, or motor and silently claim that the whole has improved. The existing simple sensor/actuator coupling has already shown mechanical effect; the remaining bottleneck is plausibly the *expressive body↔material action space* and discoverability of multiple meaningful continuations in a common world. That is a **hypothesis**, not an architecture verdict.

Return to the original competing **world-first / anatomy-first / coupling-first / workbench-first** explanations, with negative controls including a matched rigid surrogate and a second actor whose later action is genuinely possible, not just displaced or accelerated. Prefer a coherent exploratory *whole* over another narrow artifact chain. X0 may be kept as a replaceable donor. Owner-observed quality continues to outrank all machine results.

## Why after K2
W2 combined 20 moving organisms with body morphing, but actual source pixels remained traffic-like; K1/K2 found real kinetic contacts but passive rigid shielding matched hinged outcomes. K2 revealed physically ungrounded torso counterrotation. Rather than another shield or scent tuning, P0 builds one freely editable 40×24m *material courtyard* with a real pivot gate, two dividing walls with an 8m opening, independent movable loads, differently sized/massed physical bodies and a 22kg active physical arm. It compares three honest declared substrate approximations: no terrain impulse, **explicit finite Coulomb-like top-down ground reaction**, and ordinary Rapier damping. Owner can select bodies, drive, brace/flow, swing limb, spawn, throw at actual collider point, pause/edit, pan/zoom, single-step.

**Nonclaims:** all body root movement is a clearly acknowledged finite externally injected top-down drive proxy, NOT feet/gait; finite terrain impulses are an explicit virtual world reaction, **not** actual contact support, normals, foot choice, locomotor morphology or evidence that 2D is preferable to grounded 3D. No NPC mind, automatic actor goals, cognition or spontaneous independent encounters. The scene is an abstract collider workbench with more relational material possibilities, not yet compelling gameplay.

## Negative lessons from P0 source work
1. Passing 32 cases did not mean gate pivot physically works; zero-gate-motion falsifier forced actual correction.
2. Calling a target-angle-zero joint **passive** was unsafe; native motor disabled until explicitly enabled.
3. A hinge can be mathematically valid yet mechanically unable to move due self-contact; preserve collision and redesign genuine geometry.
4. Sufficiently high virtual terrain flow friction immobilized the broad operator's root motor. Reduced FLOW opposing force; posture does not hard-code `canMove=false`. Do not trade user motion feel for large reaction counters.
5. A contact impulse measured solely at first body's root falsely reported ZERO for a hinged lever whose arm struck the gate. Include all actual jointed parts.
6. A sequence of operator actions that physically changes the environment is NOT autonomous NPC planning. Moving one actor away after the first stage is an **experimental authored control**, not an uncoached world continuation.

## Strategic interpretation / next gate
P0 did establish a **new kind of scoped relationship** compared with K1/K2: actor-generated physical state of the environment changes what a *later independent material action* can achieve. This helps H-WORLD, not H-ANATOMY. A rigid/simple/small opener is a credible successful rival. P0 therefore stays isolated and provisional; do not automatically merge into main, publish, ask Owner to test prematurely or convert into mandatory P1/P2 roadmap.
